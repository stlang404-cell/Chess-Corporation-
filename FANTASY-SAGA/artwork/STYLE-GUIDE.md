# SERIES VISUAL STYLE GUIDE — *The Long Survey*

**How the art was made.** No image-generation model was available in this environment. All artwork is **original procedural vector art**, written as SVG by `production/art-src/make_art.py` and rasterized with Chromium by `render_art.js`. It copies no existing design. The SVG sources are included, so every piece can be edited or re-rendered at any resolution.

## Visual identity
- **Concept:** a surveyor's world at the edge of order. Ruled graticules and brass meridian lines (holding) are set against wandering contour lines (the Drift).
- **Palette:**
  - Night ink `#07111c` to `#10283a`
  - Drift teal `#2f6f73`
  - Dawn gold `#e6c27f`
  - Brass `#d9b56a`
  - Pale gold type `#f2d896`
  - Vellum `#e9dcbb`, with sepia ink `#2b2116` for maps and interiors
- **Type:** EB Garamond (OFL), in spaced capitals for titles and italic for subtitles.
- **Motifs:**
  - the wren's foot (Ilse's mark), set in a ring like a seal
  - chain ticks along rules
  - the dotted brass meridian
  - lit windows in leaning buildings
  - the sounder breaching from land
  - the skein (an airborne river loop)
- **Covers:** a dark gradient sky with a dawn band. One wonder image per book, a graticule at about 13% opacity, a thin double gold border, the title block at the top, and the author at the foot.

## Honest assessment
The cover and maps are clean and professional in feel. The interior vignettes are simple, naive line drawings rather than finished illustration. A professional illustrator could use these compositions as layout guides.
