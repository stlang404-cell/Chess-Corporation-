# CHARACTER BIBLE

## ILSE MARROW — protagonist
- **Age 23.** Born 1180 H. in Wick Fallow, the Fallowmarch. Close third-person POV for all of Book One.
- **Appearance:** small and wiry, with dark straight hair cut short with her own knife. Ink-stained right forefinger. Grey-green eyes. A scar across her left palm from a chain-link (age 14). She dresses in practical secondhand clothes and owns one good coat, her father's.
- **Biography:**
  - Her family worked eleven acres of leased-then-purchased land called **Marrow's Long Field**.
  - **The ghost (wound):** when she was 12 (1192 H.), Lord Saffery's licensed surveyor, **Corwin Fask**, re-charted the parish. Within latitude, the Long Field's boundary moved *sixty yards*, so that the family's well and best soil fell inside Saffery land. It was legal, fully attested and upheld by the Concordance Court on appeal. Her father **Daun Marrow** spent his savings on the appeal and then worked as a hired hand on what had been his own field. He died of a fever three years later, a smaller man.
  - Her mother **Hesta** is alive, works as a laundress in Hobbet, is fierce and practical, and disapproves of Ilse's work while taking the money.
  - Ilse is levelled, with strong sensitivity and unknown capacity. At 14 she apprenticed herself to **Old Abelard Quince**, a disgraced hedge-surveyor and forger in Hobbet, who taught her measurement, blind ink, ghosting (in small ways) and the forger's maxim: "*A good lie is ninety-nine parts truth.*" Quince died in 1200 H. Since then she has worked alone, forging small boundary corrections for smallholders: a hedge here, a ditch there, always within latitude, always *restoring* what she believes was stolen. She has never been caught.
  - **Witness-mark:** she has used dead Quince's mark ghosted under her own clients' marks so that the burden goes to a dead man. She thinks of this as "clever." In Book One she has to learn the Meridian mark ritual, and she chooses a mark: a stylized **wren's foot** (three lines and a spur).
- **Personality:** precise, dry and funny under pressure. Proud and stubborn. Generous in actions, stingy with words about feelings. She notices everything about how things are made and lies easily but hates being lied *to*. She likes order, hot tea and the sound of a chain paying out. She has a forger's eye: she sees *how* a thing could be falsified before she sees what it is.
- **Desire (want):** to earn a legitimate Patent of Charting. That would make her untouchable, able to re-survey Wick Fallow legally and win back the Long Field.
- **Need:** to understand that truth isn't the same as fixity. A true map can admit motion, and she herself can change without being fraudulent.
- **Fear:** exposure. Being found out as hollow, "a forgery of a person."
- **Contradiction:** she believes maps are the only defense of the weak against the strong, and she makes them lie.
- **Flaws:** secretive, contemptuous of the comfortable, slow to trust, quick to assume she must handle everything alone, and prone to control.
- **Skills (Book One start):** expert draftsmanship and hand-measurement; an excellent sense of latitude ("feel for give"); blind ink, ghosting and erasure. Self-taught gaps: poor formal mathematics (error theory, triangulation at scale), no fieldwork in the true Drift, no legal knowledge.
- **Moral beliefs:** law serves whoever writes it. Small lies to repair large wrongs are justified. Never let someone else carry your weight (ironically, she has been letting a dead man carry it).
- **Book One arc:** from a lone forger who uses the College as a shield, to a member of a party who chooses to expose herself to save others, and who discovers the *open map*. She ends with legitimacy *on honest terms*: a recognized student of Contrary Charting under Brask. She hasn't won the field. She has won the right to keep trying, in daylight.
- **Seven-book transformation:** from forger to founder. By Book Seven she forges the Ninefold's release (one last lie in service of the truth), loses her own fixity, and becomes someone who can live in motion. She chooses home as a relationship, not a boundary.

## ODILE BRASK — mentor
- **Age 61.** Field-Surveyor Emerita. Tall and rawboned, with cropped white hair. A black leather patch over her right eye. Her **left hand is grey and stilled**, the fingers locked in a half-curl, and she wears no glove over it.
- She carries **the Long Lie**, a brass-shod surveyor's staff marked in links. She claims it is "the only honest thing in the building, and even it's a little long" (it is exactly one rod, but she calls it a lie because it is held).
- **Biography:** a Margin-born farmer's daughter (not Margin-folk, a Margin *village*: Thessaly Ford). She was the brilliant field surveyor of her generation. In 1183 H. she led the **Sallow Expedition** (7 members, including Aurek Hallam as second). They found the sounders' singing-grounds and encountered *something* (the Vey). Four members were lost: **Ansel Corrow, Mira Tey, Jory Paske and Benedek Vass.** Brask lost her eye to a hollowing. Hallam carried her back. Her testimony ("they were trying to talk to us") was officially ruled Drift-hallucination. Her reputation collapsed. She was moved to teach Fieldwork, a junior post, and has taught it for 19 years.
- She **refuses to attest new maps**. "I've signed enough of the world. It can hold itself or it can't." Her stilled hand comes from carrying the expedition's burden.
- **Personality:** dry, blunt, sardonic. Kind *only* in deeds. She asks questions instead of giving answers, and she rewards honesty about error. Underneath there is grief and a wild curiosity she keeps on a short leash. She is funny in a bone-dry way and smokes a clay pipe that she never lights indoors.
- **Teaching philosophy:** "The map is a promise, not a cage. Know which one you're making." "If you can't say how you might be wrong, you haven't measured anything." She teaches her students to *walk first and draw second.*
- **Relationship with Ilse:** she recognizes Ilse's forger's tells on the Walk survey (Ilse "corrected" a stream bank that didn't match, the way forgers do, rather than recording the anomaly). She chooses to say nothing and to take her as a Fieldwork assistant, to see what she is. She is exacting. Midbook she confronts Ilse privately: "You're a forger. Good. So was I, once — every surveyor who ever chose what to leave out." She is suspended in Ch 21 for sponsoring a suspected forger.
- **What she knows:** the Vey are real. She suspects Hallam of something since he returned from the Sallow "too certain," but she doesn't know about the ghosting.
- **Series:** Book 2 returns to the Sallow. **Book 4: she stills completely to hold a collapsing road under her students, becoming a benchmark in the Drift. She is not dead, but lost.** Book 7: she is released and spends one final day awake.

## TEODOR "TEO" ANSELM — friend
- **Age 24.** From Lisk; his family runs a chandlery and a pie shop. He is huge, soft-spoken and round-faced, with curly auburn hair and a beard that never quite fills in. Terrified of heights. A mathematical savant (he does long division in his head for comfort). He cooks constantly, using contraband spirit-stoves.
- He failed the Patent exam twice (from nerves) and took the Walk out of stubbornness. He nearly fails it until Ilse helps him (Ch 4).
- **Discipline:** Soundings. He can "hear" depth and hidden structure. Gentle, funny, honest to a fault, and a terrible liar. He's the conscience of the party.
- **Voice:** long, digressive sentences full of food metaphors and apologies; precise numbers ("forty-one and a bit"). He calls Ilse "Marrow" when worried.
- **Book One arc:** fear to courage. In Ch 28 he climbs the Tern Stack in the dark despite his terror. His fingers begin greying in Part III (he's been ghosted). He loves Hen Solberg's laugh. Teo and Cass's friendship is sibling-like and bickering.
- **Series:** becomes Master of Soundings and then Provost of the re-founded Meridian (Book 7). Badly injured in Book 5 (loses a leg below the knee in the Still City). Married to Hen by Book 6.

## CASSIA "CASS" TENTER — friend
- **Age 22.** Margin-folk of the Tenter wagon. Brown skin, freckles, and a mass of black braids tied with route-cords (knotted strings recording roads). Wiry and fast, she whistles through her teeth and carries a sling and a skinning knife.
- She took the Walk because her grandmother **Nan Ottery Tenter** told her to "go and learn what the paper-walls know, then come home and tell us." She is also the first Margin-folk student in eleven years, and is resented and fetishized in equal measure.
- **Skills:** a natural wayfinder. She reads grain, wayhorns and weather. She practices *wending* without thinking of it as magic, and she's dismissive of "charting everything." She's poor at formal draftsmanship and maths.
- **Personality:** sharp-tongued, loyal, quick to anger, quick to forgive. Funny and profane. She's openly scornful of the College while secretly loving it. Bisexual; she flirts widely and commits rarely.
- **Voice:** Margin dialect touches: "hey-ho" as a greeting; "paper-walls" for Reach people; "rent's coming due"; she drops some articles; she calls the College "the Big House"; she swears by "Road and rain."
- **Conflict:** the College suspects the Margin-folk of causing the voids (Ch 13 onward). Cass is questioned in Ch 18. Her family's camp land at **Corrow** is "pruned" (its charters redacted) by Hallam in Ch 19, which makes it personal.
- **Book One arc:** distrust to commitment. She chooses to stay and fight for the College *as it should be*. She teaches Ilse the road-song idea of a route that moves, which seeds the open map.
- **Series:** becomes the voice of the Margin. Her heritage connects to the ninth Ninefold surveyor, the one who defected (Book 4). She has a Book 3–5 romance with **Orla Devane**.

## HENRIKE "HEN" SOLBERG — fourth party member
- **Age 19.** A Patent student from the Salt Steps, the scholarship daughter of a salt-pan foreman. Small, freckled and blond, with a big laugh. Excellent at Mensuration. She chose Ilse's party because "you looked like you knew what you were doing, and the others looked like they wanted to marry each other."
- **Function:** the warm heart of the party, and the human face of the Quiet. She **goes Quiet in Ch 16.** She is listless, her eyes flat and her hands greying, because she is the party's most diligent Morning Survey signer. She's in the infirmary from Ch 18 to Ch 30. She recovers in Ch 31 when the conduit sheet is destroyed.
- **Series:** recovers fully. Becomes a Concordance advocate and Teo's wife.

## KASIMIR REYNE — rival, later ally (romance seed)
- **Age 21.** Son of Chancellor Lady Imogen Reyne. Tall and lean, with dark-blond hair always combed, and grey-blue eyes. Beautiful handwriting. A Charting prodigy: his charters hold like iron because they are flawless.
- **Biography:** raised in Tessary's highest circles. His father **Lord Matthias Reyne** stilled at 50 from bearing too much burden for the Court and stands in the Reyne family chapel. Kasimir has a private, terrible fear of stilling, which he hides behind perfectionism: if every record is flawless, there's minimal strain. He spent his summer as a junior assessor attached to a Concordance inspection in the Fallowmarch, where **he spotted Ilse's forged hedge-line** at Wick Fallow (Ch 1–2).
- **Personality:** correct, formal and fiercely principled about law. Dryly witty when off guard. He's not cruel, but he's contemptuous of carelessness. He believes rules are what protect people from the powerful, which is Ilse's own belief from the other side. He keeps a small notebook of "errors observed," his own included.
- **Voice:** complete sentences, no contractions when on his dignity, precise vocabulary; he addresses Ilse as "Miss Marrow" until Ch 24, then "Ilse" once and the reader feels it.
- **Book One arc:** He recognizes her, and keeps quiet only because he lacks proof (Ch 7). He duels her at the Moot (Ch 20) and *loses*, because her map is better at latitude. He then files a formal accusation, because she cheated *in her past*, and he can't let it go (Ch 21). He is confronted with the ghosting evidence (Ch 25), and chooses truth over the letter of the law (Ch 26). At the climax his flawless hand drafts the open map with Ilse (Ch 29–30). At the tribunal he testifies for her (Ch 31). Mutual, unspoken attraction. **No consummated romance in Book One:** one almost-moment (Ch 27, rooftop) and a hand held a beat too long (Ch 32).
- **Series:** breaks with his mother (Book 3). Lovers by Book 4. Partners.

## Faculty
- **Magister Aurek Hallam** — Master of the Register; **Book One antagonist**. Age 64. Kind-faced, bald, with a white beard and half-moon spectacles. He wears **grey kid gloves at all times** ("old Sallow frostbite"). A warm lecturer who loves the Reach, and the second on the Sallow Expedition, where he carried Brask back. He takes a fatherly interest in Ilse, gives her Register access as a "promising draftswoman," and quietly warns her that Brask is "a brilliant woman broken by grief; don't let her break you." *Secret:* see `continuity/REVELATION-LEDGER.md`. **Voice:** gentle and precise, fond of the phrase "my dear," quotes the Ninefold, and speaks of the Reach as "her," like a ship.
- **Provost Aurelian Steyne** — head of the College. Age 58. Silver-haired and elegant, a diplomat. He knows the Zero Sheets are softening (Hallam told him in 1200). He forbade publication and covers up the Quiet. He is not part of the ghosting but is complicit in the silence. Red herring. He is publicly disgraced in Ch 31, though he retains his office (Book 2 tension).
- **Master Pim Halloway** — Mensuration. Age 40. A twitchy, brilliant, kind man who stammers when lecturing about anything but numbers and is obsessed with error theory. "Error is not sin. *Unrecorded* error is sin." He wears three watches.
- **Magistra Ines Calder** — Concordance. Age 50. A severe, elegant former Court advocate with a fondness for dramatic pauses and an exact knowledge of precedent. She is fair. She runs the Moot.
- **Archivist Ruel Damask** — Palimpsest. Age 70s. A tiny, ancient, shuffling man with magnifying lenses on brass arms who smells of vinegar. He's thrilled by anyone who looks closely. He gives Ilse the "Vey script" fragment clue (Ch 32) and teaches the palimpsest lens techniques that reveal the ghosting.
- **Master Gethin Moy** — Soundings. A bass-voiced Ammonite miner's son who directs the Sounders' Choir.
- **Dr. Sabeth Orle** — Infirmary. Brisk and humane. She doesn't believe the "Drift-fatigue" story and becomes Ilse's ally (Ch 18, 23).
- **Mother Nettle** — crow-mistress. Ancient, foul-mouthed, kind.
- **Merrin Coyle** — Registrar-Adjunct. Age 35. Hallam's anxious assistant. He knows *part* of the scheme (he prepares the Morning Survey sheets on Hallam's instructions, with blind-ink under-drawings he believes are "registration guides"). He suspects; he breaks and confesses in Ch 27. He's a coward who becomes slightly brave.
- **Lisbet Arnaud** — senior proctor, fifth-year. Kasimir's cousin. Officious, then decent.

## Students (secondary)
- **Orla Devane** (Kasimir's party): a merry, sardonic Tessary gentry girl and a gossip. Later Cass's love.
- **Piety Mercer**: pious, competitive, nasty. A minor antagonist among the students.
- **Absalom Grice**: a hearty sportsman, dim but loyal to Kasimir.
- **Wil Ashdown**: a second-year who went Quiet before Book One begins (the first case Ilse sees, Ch 8).
- **Yara Penhallow**: a Walker who didn't return from the Walk (Ch 4). Her fate is unknown, and she's found alive in Book 2.

## Outside the College
- **Hesta Marrow**: Ilse's mother.
- **Lord Edric Saffery**: the Fallowmarch landowner, Ilse's adversary in the background.
- **Corwin Fask**: Saffery's surveyor, who appears in Ch 1.
- **Nan Ottery Tenter**: Cass's grandmother, the Tenter wagon matriarch, a singer of road-songs (Ch 19).
- **Lady Imogen Reyne**: the Chancellor. She appears in Ch 31.

## Relationship map (Book One end state)
- Ilse ↔ Teo: brother-and-sister closeness and total trust.
- Ilse ↔ Cass: best friends, mutual protectors and a teasing rivalry over method.
- Ilse ↔ Hen: affection and guilt (Ilse feels responsible for not seeing the Quiet sooner).
- Ilse ↔ Kasimir: rivals turned allies, with charged tension. He knows her worst thing. She knows his (the fear of stilling).
- Ilse ↔ Brask: apprentice and mentor. Respect, and the beginning of love on both sides.
- Ilse ↔ Hallam: betrayed trust. She grieves the kindness that was real.
- Teo ↔ Hen: courting by the end.
- Cass ↔ Orla: spark.
