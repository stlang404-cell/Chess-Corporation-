# CANON LOCK — fixed facts every chapter must respect

## Calendar (1203 H.)
Months, in order: Thaw, Seedtime, Blossom, Greening, Highsun, Haysel, Reaping, Gleaning, Fogmonth, Frost, Deepwinter, Candlemonth. Each has 30 days.
- **Autumn equinox:** Reaping 22, the Walk. **Midwinter:** Frost 22, Errata Night. **Spring equinox:** Thaw 22 (of 1204 H.), dawn ceremony of the Equinox Charter.
- The year number changes at Thaw 1. Book One's climax is therefore in **1204 H.** The lease expired in 1200 H.

## The Seven Laws
Witness, Fidelity, Precedence, Attestation, Burden, Latitude, Transfer. Only six are taught. Transfer is unofficial forger-knowledge.

## Measurements
Link = 7.92 in. Rod = 16½ ft = 25 links. Chain = 66 ft = 100 links = 4 rods. Furlong = 10 chains. Mile = 80 chains. League = 3 miles.
- The Walk cairn: 3 miles out (into the Margin, southwest).
- Tern Stack: 3 leagues (9 miles) west by southwest; a sea-stack about 140 feet high.
- Distance from Wick Fallow to Cape Verity: about 560 miles; four days by the express mail coach on the Great Meridian Road.
- Widow Agnes Tull's hedge: moved **two rods** (33 ft) west, restoring the orchard strip that Corwin Fask's Haysel 1203 re-survey had taken.
- Marrow's Long Field: lost **sixty yards** of frontage, including the well, in 1192 H.

## Walk statistics
19 Walkers set out. 11 returned with passing surveys. 6 returned and failed. 2 didn't return: Yara Penhallow and a man named Oskar Fell. (Fell is found alive, half-mad, in the Margin on Reaping 25.) Yara is never found in Book One.

## Party rooms

## Ilse's mark (locked after Chapter 1)
- Since Quince's death in 1200 H., Ilse has signed her forgeries *visibly* with her own **unregistered** mark, a **wren's foot** (three lines and a spur). Under it, in blind ink, she ghosts the mark of dead Abelard Quince, so the burden runs to him. Unregistered marks can't be traced to a person.
- At Benchmarking (Ch 6) she chooses to register the wren's foot as her official witness-mark, because it is the only mark that has ever felt like hers. That is a half-reckless, half-honest act. It is also how Kasimir later proves the Tull forgery is hers (Ch 21): the visible mark on the Tull charter matches her Benchmark on the Old Wall, and the ghosted mark beneath it belongs to a man dead three years before the charter’s date. Kasimir first notices the resemblance at Benchmarking (Ch 6/7) but can't prove it until he obtains the charter.
- Hold-sense: Ilse calls the pressure behind her ears "the level." Burden aches in her right (signing) hand.
- Chapter 1 details: Ilse's savings are 44 marks 6 (after the Tull fee). Saffery's steward named 60 marks for the north frontage. Tam Brewin, carter, has a bay mare. The milestone reads HOBBET II. Corwin Fask re-charted Wick Fallow in Haysel 1203, two months before Ch 1 (the Tull hedge) and in autumn 1192 (the Long Field).
