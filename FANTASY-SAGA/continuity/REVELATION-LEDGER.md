# REVELATION LEDGER *(PRIVATE — FULL SPOILERS)*

## A. Objective truth — Book One mystery ("the Unravelling and the Quiet")
1. **1200 H.:** Aurek Hallam, Master of the Register, measures *softening* in the Zero Sheets of the First Atlas on Register Level Seven. The oldest records are losing hold, by roughly a hair's breadth per season, accelerating. He doesn't know why. (Series truth: the Lease expired.) He reports to Provost Steyne. Steyne forbids disclosure, fearing that the Court would use it to seize the Register.
2. **1200–1203 H.:** Hallam acts alone. He ties his own witness-mark into the Zero Sheets as a supporting attestation. This takes enormous burden: his hands and forearms are grey, hence the gloves. He realizes he can't carry it, so he devises **the Conduit**.
3. **The Conduit:** a master folio in Hallam's private study (behind the Register Antechamber), the **Conduit Sheet**. It is drawn in blind ink, in perfect registration with the Zero Sheets, and it carries Hallam's mark as the root. From 1201 H. he has ordered (through Merrin Coyle) that all **Morning Survey folios** be printed with a faint under-drawing in blind ink. Coyle is told these are "registration guides." In fact they are *ghost-traces* that link each student's witness-mark on each Morning Survey sheet to the Conduit, and through it to the Zero Sheets. Each morning, roughly 200 students unknowingly take on a sliver of the First Atlas's burden.
4. **Effect on students:** most carry slivers without symptoms. The most diligent signers, who sign the most sheets, and those with low capacity accumulate fastest. They go **Quiet**: listless, flat-eyed, grey-handed. Cases: Wil Ashdown (2nd year, before Book One), then two others, then **Hen Solberg (Ch 16)**, then **Teo** (early greying, Ch 22 onward), plus others. Steyne and the infirmary call it "Drift-fatigue."
5. **The voids ("unravellings"):** to reduce the total strain on the Zero Sheets, Hallam **redacts** (destroys) the charters of outlying parishes and Margin settlements. He calls it "pruning": sacrificing the edges to save the heart. Where the charters are redacted, the land loses hold and *unravels* (roads come apart, hedges wander, a well moves). These are the "voids." The College and Court blame **Margin-folk sabotage** or "Drift incursion." Voids follow Hallam's "inspection tours" (he must be physically present to redact a parish charter properly, because the release must be done near the land). The Wick Fallow lane unravelling in **Ch 1** is one of these.
6. **Hollowings near the Cape:** drawn by the heavy recent attestations (Hallam's re-attestations and the dense ghost-linked Morning Survey sheets).
7. **The Equinox plan:** the ghosting is too slow, and the Zero Sheets are failing faster. At the spring equinox, all ~300 College members co-sign the **Equinox Charter** under the Lantern Gate. Hallam has prepared that folio with a full-strength ghost-trace: everyone who signs will take on a *massive* share of the Zero Sheets' burden at once. It would be enough to hold Tessary steady for perhaps ten years. The cost: most signers go Quiet, and some of the weakest still entirely. Hallam regards this as a terrible necessity. ("A few hundred of us, my dear, against a million of her children.")
8. **What stops it (Book One climax):** (a) Ilse's team wins the Long Measure with an **open charter**: a record that attests the Drift Wing's route *as a range*. It is true, so its fidelity is maximal. (b) A ghost-trace requires line-for-line registration with its target. An open charter can't register with fixed Zero lines, so a ghost under an open charter fails. (c) Under the Law of Precedence, Ilse's higher-fidelity open charter of the Drift Wing *supersedes* Hallam's prepared folio. (d) Kasimir and Cass retrieve the **Conduit Sheet** from Hallam's study during the ceremony, guided by Merrin Coyle's confession. (e) Ilse tears the Conduit. All ghost-links break. The burden carried by the students releases *back up the chain* to its root: Hallam. He stills on the steps of the Lantern Gate. (f) **Consequence:** the Zero Sheets lose their borrowed support, and the fraying in Tessary is now *worse* (the Book Two pressure). Nobody yet knows why the Zero Sheets are failing in the first place.

## C. Clue map (summary; detail in planning/MYSTERY-CLUE-MAP.md)
1. Ch 1: the lane at Wick Fallow unravels, the night after "a College gentleman in grey gloves" visited the Hobbet parish office.
2. Ch 5: Hallam's gloves; his line "old Sallow frostbite."
3. Ch 8: Wil Ashdown, a Quiet second-year, has grey fingers on his *signing hand*.
4. Ch 9: rain during Morning Survey. Ilse sees a faint second line beneath her mark on the damp folio and assumes she smudged it.
5. Ch 10: Hallam lectures on six Laws. Ilse notes silently that he skips Transfer, which "every hedge-forger in the Fallowmarch knows."
6. Ch 12: in the library, the Morning Survey paper contract changed suppliers in 1201 H. (Ilse sees the watermark change: a new mill mark, a *Register* order).
7. Ch 13: a void at Brine End. Hallam was away "on inspection" that week.
8. Ch 15: Ilse uses her own blind-ink lens and confirms ghost-lines under the Morning Survey sheets. She can't report without exposing herself.
9. Ch 16: Hen goes Quiet. She is the party's most diligent signer.
10. Ch 17: Sorry eats ink. It refuses to eat Morning Survey sheets. (Blind ink is made with gall and *quicksilver*, which ossels avoid.)
11. Ch 19: Corrow is pruned. Nan Ottery: "a gentle old man with grey hands came asking the way."
12. Ch 22: Teo's fingers begin greying.
13. Ch 23: Dr. Orle's ledger shows the Quiet cases began in 1201 H.
14. Ch 24: Damask teaches the raking-light lens. The ghost-lines run toward a single root, "like a delta reversed."
15. Ch 25: Kasimir's evidence: void dates against Register travel warrants, signed by Hallam.
16. Ch 26: Brask's memory of Hallam on the Sallow: "he came back certain. He was never certain before."
17. Ch 27: Coyle confesses about the "registration guides" and the Equinox folio.

## D. Character knowledge at Book One end
- **Ilse:** knows everything in section A except the *reason* the Zero Sheets are failing. She has found the Ninefold-script fragment and the phrase she half-translates via Sorry: "*...until the term is spent...*"
- **Brask:** the same, plus her Sallow knowledge of the Vey.
- **Kasimir:** knows the scheme. Will report to his mother, and must decide how much.
- **Steyne:** knows everything and says little.
- **Cass:** knows the scheme. Her grandmother's proverb "rent's coming due" begins to trouble her.
- **The public:** told that "a Master of the Register committed grave misconduct; the Quiet is cured." The fraying is not disclosed.
