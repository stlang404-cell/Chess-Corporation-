## Your passes, in order, for each chapter in your range
1. **Continuity.** Names, dates, ages, marks and who knows what when (`continuity/CHARACTER-KNOWLEDGE.md`). Also physical facts: Brask's patch is over her right eye and her left hand is stilled; Hallam wears gloves; Sorry's eyes are amber. Check objects, injuries and weather. Fix contradictions with other chapters.
2. **Character.** Voices must be distinct and consistent with the voice guide. Motivations must be believable. Ilse must have agency. Hallam must stay warm and sympathetic until Ch 25.
3. **Worldbuilding and magic.** Every use of hold must obey the Seven Laws (Witness, Fidelity, Precedence, Attestation, Burden, Latitude, Transfer). No new powers. Use the canonical terms and spellings.
4. **Mystery.** Clues must appear no earlier or more obviously than `planning/MYSTERY-CLUE-MAP.md` allows, and any clue your range is supposed to plant must actually be there.
5. **Developmental tightening.** Remove repeated emotional beats and redundant exposition, especially lore re-explained in several chapters. Make sure each scene changes something.
6. **Line edit.** Rhythm, clarity, freshness of imagery, and dialogue that sounds like speech. Cut generic generated-fiction phrasing. Book-wide overuse to reduce in your range:
   - "for a long moment" and "for a long time": at most 2 each in your range
   - "half-moon spectacles": at most 1 in your range
   - "exactly": at most 5 in your range
   - "slowly": at most 6 in your range
   - "something": cut by about a third
   - "the way a [noun]" similes: cut by about a third
   - "the level in her ears": at most 2 in your range
   - "a long moment" and "very still": at most 2 each
   - Also watch for any image or simile that repeats across chapters.
7. **Copyedit.** Grammar, punctuation, British-flavored spelling ("grey", "colour"), curly quotes, em dashes, and consistent capitalization of terms.

## Rules
- Edit the chapter files directly. Keep each chapter **3,200–3,950 words** (check with `wc -w`). Keep the heading format.
- Don't rewrite for the sake of it. The draft is strong, so preserve its voice and its best lines. Make targeted, meaningful improvements.
- Don't change plot events or outcomes. If you find a plot-level problem you can't fix locally, report it.
- Write your report to `editorial/REVIEW-chNN-chMM.md`. It should list, per chapter, the concrete problems found (with quotes or line references) and what you changed, plus any unresolved issues for the lead editor. Then add an **"Adversarial notes"** section with your candid view on your range as (a) a demanding developmental editor, (b) a skeptical adult fantasy reader, and (c) a continuity and mystery specialist, with chapter references.
- Don't touch other files or git.
