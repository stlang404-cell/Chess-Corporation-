## Chapter 7 — "Fieldwork" (Gleaning 1–2)

**Summary.** First Fieldwork class in the leaking shed at the Margin end of the First Range, with mismatched buckets under four leaks. Brask has each student estimate the distance to a gorse bush and say how they might be wrong. Ilse's answer is the best (3 ch 10 l, give or take 6, with the hidden fold, the bush's size and the flat light named as sources of error). The chained distance is 3 ch 22 l, and Brask tells her that being out by twice her stated error is "the worst sort of wrong". After class she makes Ilse her unpaid Fieldwork assistant (chains, tallow, levelglasses). Brask is visibly exhausted: she found Oskar Fell three days out, in a salt-pool, counting his fingers, and she won't answer about Yara. In Charting under Master Pell, Kasimir's cobble-charter holds like iron, Ilse's moves "a hair and a half", Piety sneers at Walkers and Orla teases Kasimir. Ilse knowingly refuses to "lean" her drawing. In the Library of Record, Kasimir reports that Jem Rudd says the Tull amendment is in order but won't break the Court seal, so the inquiry must be refiled with the Board (it sits in Frost) and may take until spring. They argue law as a wall versus law as the powerful's word. He says the locked line ("I do not dislike you..."). She keeps the Long Field to herself. In the evening on the Wren Stair there is Teo's contraband stove and fried sausage, Cass's charm story, Hen's laugh, and Sorry drinking Teo's Lisk ink. Brask's note comes under the door: "Walk with me tomorrow, Marrow. Before the bell." End of Part One.

**State at end.** Night of Gleaning 2. The party is in the Wren Stair. Ilse is Brask's Fieldwork assistant. She knows Kasimir has no proof yet and suspects he connected her Benchmark mark with the Tull flourish. The note is in her father's coat pocket. The wardrobe is 11 in. from the wall.

**Invented details.** Master Wystan Pell, Hallam's Charting adjunct (narrow, pink-handed, slight stammer, invokes Hallam's standards). The cobble-on-sand charting exercise, tested with a mallet. The Library of Record's tiled floor map of the Reach, and the Board procedure book *Concordance Procedure, Practice of the Board*, with its "such use as the College saw fit" clause, which foreshadows Ch 31. Teo's three spirit-stoves (two-mark fine), "emergency mustard", Anselm's pie shop (109 years), his grandmother who threw a tax-assessor into the canal. Hen's father tastes the salt-pans. Hen pencils the wardrobe gap inside its door each morning. Cass's charm-seller in Verity Cross.

**Deviations.** Kasimir's reply was adjusted to fit Ch 2, where he sealed the chest under Court seal. The clerk (Jem Rudd) declines to break the seal rather than calling for "no further inquiry". The outline's "the parish replied the charter is 'in order'" is kept.

## Chapter 8 — "The Quiet" (Gleaning 3)

**Summary.** A pre-dawn walk on the Margin with Brask. Gorse tips lean toward hold. Dew sits still on held stone and trembles on unheld peat. The Long Lie rings on held stone and thuds in peat, and the level's pitch changes with direction (the grain). Ilse walks 50 paces eyes-shut and comes out 11 off ("Last week you'd have been in the sea"). Ilse sees that Brask's stilled hand can't grip or release; Brask says "Don't." Asked why she came, Ilse tells the half-truth of the Long Field, and Brask makes no comment. On Yara: "The Margin keeps what it keeps." Later, returning Dr. Orle's calipers and fine chain (lent for Fell), Ilse meets Wil Ashdown on an infirmary bench: flat eyes, the right fingers slate-grey, the pen-forefinger darkest. She recognizes burden-grey from Quince's last years and finds it impossible at 21. She glimpses a second Quiet student (an unnamed furlong girl). Orle mutters that it isn't fatigue, "whatever the Provost likes to call it." At supper Steyne announces "seasonal Drift-fatigue... rest and broth." Cass says "Broth. Road and rain." Wil is led out of the Mess. Hallam, gloved, eats almost nothing and is visibly saddened. At night Ilse practises the wren's foot and feels how much more hers it is than Quince's mark ever was.

**State at end.** Ilse knows the Quiet exists, that it greys the signing hand and that Orle doubts the official story. She has not connected it to Morning Survey. A page of practice marks is in a hidden pocket she sewed into the lining of her father's coat (set-up for Ch 15's forger's kit). Sorry is on her feet.

**Invented details.** Orle's lent calipers and fine chain (Brask took a kink out of link 43). Quince's mark described as a knot (a loop doubled back with a tail). Quince's hands greyed in his last two years, and he tied his pen on with thread ("That's the bill coming in"). Ilse's wren's-foot stroke order. The second Quiet patient is a furlong girl with a plait (unnamed). Hen's gran's brine-pan "cure". A furlong heckles the Provost: "Is that the cure or the cause?"

**Deviations.** None of substance. Brask releases Ilse at the bell for Morning Survey. The infirmary errand comes later in the morning.

## Chapter 9 — "Rain on the Register" (Gleaning 10)

**Summary.** Morning Survey in rain. The Long Gallery leaks through its leaded roof and is 2½ links long (1 ch 96½ l against 1 ch 94 l), and the Wren Stair has grown a 31st tread. The stretch reads 3 ch 44 l instead of 3 ch 41 l. Sorry wails when left and rides in Ilse's coat. In the Antechamber, Coyle (ink-dyed cuffs, sweet milky tea) gives out the four stretch folios and reminds them that Hallam likes sheets signed there. Hen volunteers to sign extra sheets ("I'm quickest"), a seed for Ch 16. After Ilse signs the Gallery East, Sorry knocks Coyle's tea across the folios. Under her wet wren's foot, a faint pale violet thread runs to the sheet's edge: the colour of blind ink. Coyle blots it invisible. On the stair she talks herself out of it (a smudge thinned with tea, plus a forger's habit of seeing blind ink). In Hallam's Principles of Hold lecture she holds an original 803 H. charter of the Ammonite Hills and feels four centuries of agreement humming in it. Hallam: "Every map is an act of love..." Afterwards he offers her Register copying two evenings a week, paid, with access to Levels One to Three. He praises her eye and gently warns that Brask is "a brilliant woman, broken by grief... Don't let her break you." She tells Brask while they tallow chains. After a long silence: "Mind the stairs."

**State at end.** Ilse has accepted the copyist post, starting the week after next. She has a suppressed memory of the violet line. She has a little over four marks.

**Invented details.** The Long Gallery has a leaded-glass roof. The four Wren Stair folios (*Wren Stair, Second Range*; *Landing*; *Long Gallery, East*; *Long Gallery, West*) each take one mark. Hen signs two most days. The far Antechamber door leads to the lower levels and Hallam's study. Ottilie Vance, the surveyor of the Ammonite charter of 803 H. (the Delve and Corbel Edge, kept on Level Four), whose margin is crowded with re-attestations. Hallam kept an ossel as a student that ate a treatise on precedence. Copyist pay is half a mark an evening, which is consistent with Ch 12's "four marks a month".

**Deviations.** Hallam's line is "the children of the Reach" rather than a named town, so as not to add an unlisted Hobbet clue.

## Chapter 10 — "The Six Laws" (Gleaning 14–20)

**Summary.** Hallam lectures the Laws on the slate: Witness, Fidelity, Precedence, Attestation, Burden ("A good charter is light"), Latitude ("You cannot chart a mountain into a lake"). He calls them six. Ilse waits for the seventh. In memory, Quince's kitchen: Transfer, which "every hedge-forger in the Fallowmarch knew". She can't tell whether the College doesn't know it or doesn't say it. Kasimir asks about contrary records. Hallam answers that they hold by Fidelity like any other record: "Forgery is not a law of nature. It is a failure of character." Everyone laughs, and Ilse burns. She writes "7. Transfer" and inks it out. In Mensuration, Halloway (three watches, "Today's Disagreement: eleven seconds") sets five measures: 4.12, 4.15, 4.11, 4.14, 4.13 chains. Ilse throws out 4.15 by instinct, doesn't know what a residual is, and stalls at the square root while Piety supplies it. Halloway finishes (mean 4 ch 13 l ± 0.48 probable error) and later tells her: "Error is not sin... Unrecorded error. That is the only kind that kills anyone." At the Errata Society in the Mess kitchen, Teo explains squaring with pie crust (raw is more than twice as bad as chewy) and n−1 as the crust burned testing the oven. Hen leans close and Teo drops the rolling pin. Cass brings cider and argues that maps are theft. Ilse: "It's only theft the first time... After that, every map is just a receipt." Alone on Gleaning 20, Ilse does thirty problems, gets twenty-six right, and annotates each of her four errors.

**State at end.** Ilse is privately alert that the College omits Transfer, and is still not suspicious of Hallam in particular. Her error theory is improving.

**Invented details.** Halloway's "Today's Disagreement" board heading. The five-measure problem and its figures (residuals −1, +2, −2, +1, 0; sum of squares 10; s = 1.581; PE single ≈ 1.07; PE of mean ≈ 0.48). Dunstan Moss, a large mild furlong who chairs the Errata Society with a ladle. Motion: "That a map is a theft" (declared a draw "on account of the pie"). Ilse's past clients include the Hedleys' ditch and old Brack's gatepost. Quince on Transfer: "the Big College would sooner nobody knew it could be done."

**Deviations.** None.

## Chapter 11 — "The Ladder" (Gleaning 28)

**Summary.** The Ladder is a windowless tower in the Third Range: six stacked rooms ("rungs") off an iron ladder shaft. Ilse leaves a penny in a Crypt benchmark's hand first. Lisbet Arnaud searches the party but ignores Cass's hair, so Cass smuggles in her route-cords. Halloway gives the rules: one chain, folding rule (brass studs every inch), plumb-line, sounding-mallet, board, pins, pen, ink, chalk; two hours; no light; the latitude is above a rod. In Rung Five, Hen counts seconds aloud for two hours, logging every length with its count. Teo sounds the walls and finds a hearth niche and a filled doorway. Cass reads the floor-grain, and Ilse chains by touch, reading the chain's tallies with her fingers. Because angles can't be sighted in the dark, Ilse uses pure trilateration (sides 52/38/52/38 links, diagonal BD 66). She builds the figure at one inch to five links with knotted route-cords pinned to the board. The check diagonal AC (63 against a predicted 62¾) closes. At count 3,041 the room groans and racks into a lozenge: the sides stay the same and BD grows to 69, then 70, then 71, a link every 600 counts. Ilse is tempted to chart it back to square, which would be the stream bank again, and refuses. Instead they choose a signing count (6,900) and predict the room's state at that instant, with Teo using the parallelogram law (AC² + BD² = 8,296) to check AC. They prick-transfer the string figure to paper and ink it by touch, adding the annotation "BD lengthens one link in each ten minutes. Recorded." Cass: "Ground likes being told what it's doing," a thought Ilse can't catch. The final check matches the prediction, all four sign at 6,900 and the room stops. Halloway is ecstatic ("You wrote down how fast you were wrong"). Results: Kasimir's party first (held to ⅕ link: he chained everything twice, fast, and fought it), Ilse's second (⅖ link, "with commendation for the rate annotation", "Best result by a Walker party since 1189"). Kasimir: "I fought it. You recorded it... Interesting." At the Chain & Anchor, Teo dances with Hen, and Cass dances with a Lisk furlong, Orla Devane and a fisherman. Ilse is happy, notices it "the way you notice you have walked out onto ice", and Hen drags her up to dance.

**State at end.** Night of Gleaning 28, at the Chain & Anchor, Verity Cross. There are no injuries (Ilse's fingertips are sore from pins). Cass has lost three route-cords at the dance. Ilse has a commendation from Halloway. The open-map seed is planted but not recognized.

**Invented details.** The Ladder's architecture (a six-rung tower, oak doors lined with black felt, barred from outside). Rung Five belongs to Ilse's party and Rung Two to Kasimir's. The room's geometry (52 × 38 links, a stone pillar, a hearth niche, a filled doorway). The racking rate and all the numbers above. Hen counted brine-pans for her father from age six. The route-cord "road to Corrow", which Ilse must not cut. The board heading "Best result by a Walker party since 1189". Halloway has set the Ladder for eleven years. The coins-for-benchmarks visit in the Crypt is shown briefly.

**Deviations.** Kasimir's room also racked. His win comes from speed and re-measurement, which sharpens the "fought it / recorded it" contrast for Ch 20 and Ch 29. Teo dances with Hen exactly as outlined. Cass's "three people" are specified, and Orla is included as a light seed for the later Cass/Orla spark.
