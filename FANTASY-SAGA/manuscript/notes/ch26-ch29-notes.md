## Chapter 26 — "What the Sallow Gave Back" (Candlemonth 22, 1203 H.)

**Summary.** Two days after the crow-loft (Ch 25), Ilse and Kasimir walk the cart road to Verity Cross after Evening Bell, without lanterns so the porter won't log them. At Brask's cottage, Kasimir shows his void-dates-against-travel-warrants page and Ilse shows raking light on a fresh Morning Survey sheet. She confesses that she told Hallam about the ghost-lines in Deepwinter. Brask works it out from the gloves ("Old Sallow frostbite", though they came home off the Sallow in Highsun and his hands were pink for twenty years). She gives the key memory: Hallam was "the best doubter I ever worked with", and he came back from the Sallow *certain* and never doubted again. She frames it as grief and fury held flat. In the strategy scene they admit they are the three least credible witnesses in the College (suspended Field-Surveyor, accused forger, Chancellor's son) against forty years of kindness. Ilse reasons that Hallam needs mass signatures, and Brask names the Equinox Charter: Thaw 22, every member signing one folio that Hallam prepares. The Long Measure's winning charter is the basis ("Twenty-nine days," says Kasimir). Brask tells Ilse to stay civil and "give him a student". On the leads above the Library of Record, Kasimir tells her more about his father's stilling and his own fear of being still. Ilse tells him she's afraid she is hollow, "forgery all the way down." He calls her "Ilse" once, then says something other than what he was about to say: nobody hollow could have drawn the Tull forgery so well. They don't kiss. The chapter ends with them laughing and hiding below the parapet.

**State at end.** Ilse, Kasimir and Brask all know Hallam is behind the voids and the ghosting. They don't yet know the Zero Sheets are softening, or about Coyle's "guides" and the Conduit Sheet by name. They suspect the Equinox Charter is the target. Brask remains suspended and barred from the grounds. Kasimir's accusation still stands, and the tribunal is still set for after the equinox. Ilse is keeping up a civil face to Hallam. No injuries.

**Invented details.**
- Brask's cottage is the last on the cliff lane at Verity Cross: whitewashed, a yard of lobster pots, books propping furniture, a cairn-crow feather as a bookmark, a spirit-lamp and a chipped brown teapot.
- Lanterns on the cart road after Evening Bell are logged at the porter's lodge.
- The Sallow party came home in Highsun 1183. Hallam began wearing gloves "three winters back" (c. 1200), consistent with the ledger. Brask spent six weeks in the infirmary, and Hallam sat with her daily.
- Hallam before the Board neither called Brask mad nor backed her. He spoke of the duty to hold. He became Master of the Register in 1189 ("six years later").
- Hallam at thirty called the First Atlas "the finest guess anyone ever made."
- Kasimir's father bore for the Court on the Lisk reclamations. He began stilling when the Ambry rose in 1193, when Kasimir was eleven, and stands in the chapel at Reyne House. Kasimir visited him daily for a year, then stopped. Kasimir has attested 94 records at the College.
- There is a ladder from the bell-chamber to the leads above the Library of Record. Kasimir's cousin Lisbet (the proctor) showed him.
- Image: low stars over the Drift rocking on the moving horizon "like lamps on a boat."

**Deviations.** None of substance. The rooftop is the Old Court roof (the leads above the Library of Record) as the outline says. The character bible puts the rooftop almost-moment at Ch 27, but I followed the outline (Ch 26).

## Chapter 27 — "Registration Guides" (Thaw 12–20, 1204 H.)

**Summary.** On Thaw 12 Mother Nettle lures Merrin Coyle to the crow-loft with a fake "crow from Tessary." Cass shuts the hatch and sits on it. Ilse shows him raking light, and he recognizes his own "registration guides." Hallam gave him a second plate and blind ink in 1201 when the paper changed, calling it a guide-print "so as not to distract the students". Coyle had noticed that the guides sit only under marks. Teo shows his grey fingertips, and Coyle breaks. Hallam took the Equinox folio blank on Candlemonth 3 and worked on it alone for three nights. There is a "master sheet" under glass in the press by the study window, which Hallam calls the Conduit. Carrying the lamp to Level Seven, Coyle has heard Hallam muttering "a hair a season… two hairs, this winter," that she is coming apart at the centre, and that the Provost won't let him say so. "He's trying to save Tessary." Coyle agrees to help. In the Mess kitchen on Thaw 14, Brask's crow-note arrives ("Aurek would never do this for less than the world… Win. Draw it so it can't be ghosted."). Kasimir raises the case for Hallam (a million in Tessary). Teo hates that he can do the sum, and Cass answers it with Corrow and the edges ("what do you eat in year eleven?"). Ilse: "Then we find another way to hold her. We don't pay for it with Hen." They settle the three-part plan and the short-party problem: three runners, plus one hour. Ilse visits Hen on Thaw 18 ("Three is a bad number for a chain… Cass should lead"). The Lantern Count follows on Thaw 20. There is an image of the procession seen from the Wren Stair window as a road made of people. Cass hums the Corrow song under her count, and Ilse half-grasps a thought (road-songs, the Ladder) and loses it. Kasimir passes going the other way and lifts his lantern an inch.

**State at end.** Ilse knows everything about the scheme except the reason for the softening: the guides, the Equinox folio, the Conduit Sheet in the study press, and the Zero Sheets softening, which Steyne has suppressed. Coyle is an ally. Brask is informed by crow. Ilse's party will run as three under the penalty, and Kasimir stays with his own party. The plan: (1) win; (2) draft a charter a ghost can't take, method still unknown to Ilse; (3) Cass, Kasimir and Coyle seize the Conduit during the ceremony. Hen is still Quiet, grey to the wrists. Teo's fingertips are slate-grey to the second knuckle.

**Invented details.**
- The crow-loft is in the roof-space above the porter's lodge and holds 112 crow-cairns. Mother Nettle has a running joke about "the Provost's hat" (crows).
- Coyle has served Hallam eleven years. He saw Hallam's hands "grey to the wrist… like stone" in Frost 1203.
- **Plant:** Hallam was short with Coyle only once, when Coyle moved the sounder-ivory paperweight to dust under it ("Please don't"). This sets up the key-in-the-paperweight reveal in Ch 30. Ilse "filed that."
- Teo made onion tart in the Mess kitchen, and Kasimir knows the scullery door.
- The Long Measure articles: the fourth article sets a party at four, and the short-party penalty adds one hour to a party's time. Last year's winners took a little under twenty hours.
- Lantern Count mechanics: a proctor lights a taper from the keystone lamp and hands it to the Provost, and the flame passes hand to hand. Students go out two by two and start counting under the keystone. The Wing is 1,306 paces at "the founders' stride." Everyone counts their own steps, so the numbers overlap "like rain." The procession goes to the far end of the Third Range and back.
- Orla and Cass exchange a flirtatious jab while lighting lanterns (seeds their later romance).

**Deviations.**
- I added a short Hen scene (Thaw 18). It isn't in the outline. It keeps Hen's stakes present and gives Cass the lead role in Ch 28 through Hen's advice.
- Ilse is present for Coyle's capture. Cass and Teo trap him, but Ilse questions him, to keep close third on Ilse.
- Coyle doesn't mention a lock or a key. That is left for Ch 30.

## Chapter 28 — "The Long Measure" (Thaw 21)

**Summary.** At a quarter to six, Calder reads the articles under the Lantern Gate: 47 students in 12 parties, with Ilse's party running as three under the penalty ("No, Magistra. But we're running."). Hallam goes down the line and says to Ilse alone, "Come home safe, my dear." She cannot tell whether there's a second line under it. Brask stands on a rise just outside the grounds and lifts the Long Lie once. Cass leads, Teo holds the back end and Ilse drafts, setting and attesting station-pins (playing-card charters on iron spikes). The Drift set pieces: standing rain on a hill, chained through and closed with a recorded two-link error. A skein arching 20 ft with skein-eels, which Ilse notes as "the bed of a stream, not its course". A warm fold full of fold-moths. The ringing glasswrack fields. There, hollowings swarm on the fresh station-pins of every party, and an Ammonite party abandons its board and turns back. Cass leads them onto a sliding tongue of sand, and the hollowing will not cross onto moving ground. Ilse notes the slide's rate on her sheet and half-has the thought again. They reach the Tern Stack first at nightfall. A posted notice says the cap is fractured and partly hollow. Teo insists on climbing because only he can *sound* the rock. He climbs 140 ft in the dark, counting holds aloud and knocking to listen, with Cass calling the grain and Ilse belaying the line round a rock horn. He re-attests the cairn, and the beacon blazes. ("Two hundred and three… I still don't like them.") Kasimir's party arrives forty minutes later and exchanges a wary nod. Cass warns them about the hollow left side. Heading home, Ilse finds their last pin eleven yards south of its mark and the whole ridge sliding. Cass: "Ground's going home without us."

**State at end.** It is night on Thaw 21. Ilse, Teo and Cass are first at the Stack and heading home with the cairn charter (Teo's mark). Ilse's hands are rope-burned and bleeding from belaying. Teo is exhausted and exultant, and his fingertips are still grey. Sorry rides in Ilse's coat. Kasimir's party is at the Stack behind them. The Drift ground along the course is moving, and hollowings are abundant. Ilse has noticed, but not yet put together, that hollowings avoid moving ground and that her own notes on the skein and the slide record motion.

**Invented details.**
- The beacon cairn has been re-attested yearly since 211 H. Calder cites *Penwarden's Party v. the Proctors*, 1131.
- Station-pins: an iron spike with a playing-card-sized charter wired on, each firming about a yard of ground around it. Ilse set 40+ on the outward route.
- Cass's "Uncle Pell" says the standing rain has fallen since Candlemonth.
- The Tenting belief that a skein-eel's touch means you'll never drown.
- The proctors' notice at the Stack's foot says the cap was fractured in the Frost storms and is hollow on one side. Teo finds it hollow on the left and signs on the right.
- There is a rope-worn horn of rock at the Stack's foot for belaying, and Cass ties a "Tenting knot with a twist."
- Teo calls Ilse "Ilse" rather than "Marrow" when he goes past fear into resolve. This is deliberate and flagged in the text.

**Deviations.** One party turning back is shown as an Ammonite party abandoning its board (left unnamed). Cass doesn't climb, because the climb needs Soundings, which justifies Teo's ascent within the magic and discipline rules. The ending is mid-motion, with a line of dialogue rather than a one-line ominous paragraph.

## Chapter 29 — "The Open Map" (Thaw 21–22, night)

**Summary.** Around midnight Ilse's pins are drifting with the ridge and hollowings are feeding on them. They hear Kasimir's party, who passed them on a straighter line, trapped in a fold between two ridges moving in opposite directions. They choose to go, giving up any chance of winning. Absalom has a broken left leg. Piety screams that the Walkers' "wagon songs" did it, and Orla holds two lanterns. Kasimir has attested nine point charters, and each one tore, with pewter-grey dulling his right knuckles. Cass tries to sing and her voice fails. She then describes Sorry as "the biggest, ugliest, fiercest dog that ever walked the Margin," and Sorry becomes a wolfhound-sized dog, drives off the hollowing, then collapses back into a tiny grey shape, asleep and shivering. Watching the tenth charter tear, Ilse deduces the open map. Under the Law of Fidelity (accuracy *and completeness*), a point charter is true for one instant and wrong the rest of the time. The strain is that wrongness. It flows as burden and draws the hollowings. The seeds converge: the Ladder's rate annotation, the Moot ("fidelity to the ground"), Nan's "song says where a thing goes" and the Corrow road-song, Brask's "promise, not a cage", her own skein and slide notes, and the Walk's corrected stream bank ("Forgers correct. Surveyors record."). So you chart the range, not the state. Such a record holds by Fidelity, beats narrower records by Precedence and costs almost nothing by Burden. It's "a law she had been taught in her first month, followed all the way to the end." They draft it together. Teo sounds and times the swing (41 s north to south, 43 s back). Cass reads the hinge (east). Ilse feels the limits of the give and posts Orla and Piety with lanterns at them. Kasimir sights and draws the bounds. Ilse and Kasimir attest, and the ground settles into a gentle anchored sway. The hollowings drift off, and Cass laughs. Ilse, thinking as a forger, sees that a ghost needs line-for-line registration with fixed lines, and an open charter has none: "A ghost needs the lines to sit still." They redraw the whole return open, including the Drift Wing causeway. At 5:20 a.m. on Thaw 22 the merged seven reach the Gate, the only party to finish. Halloway is ecstatic ("it's the rate annotation"). Steyne objects that seven isn't four and that a charter fixes a place. Calder rules: "A charter records what is." By the Law of Fidelity it is true, it holds, it stands, and it will be the basis of the Equinox Charter. Hallam reads it long and feels along a bound "for a door he knows is there", finds none, and for a moment looks like the old doubter. Then he says, "Remarkable… my dear. Get some sleep. We have a long morning ahead of us."

**State at end.** It is about 5:30 a.m. on Thaw 22, under the Lantern Gate, with the ceremony at dawn. The open route charter, including the Drift Wing drawn open, has been accepted as the basis of the Equinox Charter and handed to Hallam. All seven signed its final sheet, and Ilse and Kasimir drafted and attested the open sections. Ilse knows an open charter defeats ghosting, and Kasimir knows too. Hallam understands what the charter does. Injuries: Absalom's left leg is broken and splinted with Kasimir's spare rule (he needs setting). Kasimir's right hand is burdened and greyish at the knuckles, wrapped in his scarf; it should ease, since the open charter carries almost no strain, but Ch 30–31 may show residual grey. Ilse's hands are rope-burned from Ch 28. Sorry is asleep, deeply, as for a large form, which may last about a day per CREATURES.md. Teo's fingertips are still grey. Five parties came in without completed routes, four are still out with proctors searching, and the Ammonite party returned at sunset. The plan to seize the Conduit stands.

**Invented details.**
- The fold mechanics: two ridges sliding in opposite directions, a hinge to the east, and a 41/43-second swing (slower going north, "uphill").
- Kasimir's nine (then ten) torn charters.
- Sorry's dog form: wolfhound-sized (within the fourfold mass limit, but "seemed much bigger"), with amber eyes.
- Calder: "eleven years of this course."
- Steyne is at the Gate in a dressing gown under his gown.

**Deviations and craft notes.**
- The deduction is built strictly from the Laws as stated in MAGIC-SYSTEM.md §3 and §9: Fidelity's "accuracy and completeness", Latitude as "the range of states", then Precedence and Burden. Every seed listed in the brief is used explicitly. Two new seeds planted in Ch 28 (the skein note and the slide rate) let Ilse discover she had already drawn "open" lines. No new power is introduced, and Ilse herself says it isn't a new law.
- Hallam's final line is gentle on the surface. The menace comes only through what the reader now knows. He is not unmasked here.
- Ilse hasn't yet thought through what happens to the Zero Sheets if the Conduit is broken. That is left for Ch 30.
