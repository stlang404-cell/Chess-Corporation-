## Rules
- Each chapter is **3,300–3,900 words**. Verify with `wc -w` and expand or trim until it's in range.
- The format is exactly as in the style guide: `# Chapter N`, then `## Title`, then the prose. Scene breaks are `* * *` on their own line. Use curly quotes and real em dashes.
- Close third person on Ilse only. Past tense.
- Dramatize in scene. Show surveying craft concretely. Include at least one image a reader could paint in each chapter. Allow quiet scenes to breathe. Give the humor to the characters.
- Vary chapter endings. Don't end every chapter on an ominous one-liner.
- Don't contradict canon. If you must invent a minor detail (a name, a room, a dish), keep it modest and consistent with the bible, and **record it** in the notes file.
- Avoid the banned phrases. Avoid repeating the same metaphor across chapters.
- Characters don't know things before they learn them. Track what Ilse knows.
- Write each chapter to `manuscript/chapters/chNN.md` (two digits: ch02.md, ch10.md).
- When finished, write `manuscript/notes/chNN-chMM-notes.md` for your range. It should hold, per chapter: a 5–8 sentence summary of what happens; the state at the end (where people are, what Ilse knows, injuries, objects); any invented names or details; and any deviations from the outline, with your reasons.
- Then self-review each chapter against the style guide (banned phrases, voice and continuity) and revise. Report back a short list: chapter, word count, and one-line summary.
- Don't modify any other files. Don't touch git.
