# BOOK THREE — *THE PALIMPSEST COURT*
*The Long Survey, Book Three. Spoilers for Books One–Two.*

## Premise
Ilse's third year (she is a "furlong"). The Concordance Court brings the *Bill of Register* before the Reach Assembly. It would abolish collegiate privilege, outlaw Contrary Charting and range-charting, and move the Register from Cape Verity to Tessary. Ilse, now the most notorious student in the Reach, is named in the bill. Meanwhile, with Archivist Damask and the script stone, she begins to decode the Ninefold's marginalia, and what she reads changes the meaning of the world.

## Themes
Law versus justice. Palimpsest: every record is written over an older one. Who has the right to read the past.

## Major acts
1. **The Bill (Thaw–Blossom 1205).** Lady Reyne's campaign. Ilse is summoned to testify in Tessary. The city of copper roofs is shown fully for the first time: its rings, its Zero Stone plaza, and the first stilled districts where citizens are going grey from bearing for hire. Kasimir must choose between his mother's house and the College.
2. **The Stacks (Greening–Haysel).** Ilse, Damask, Kasimir and Sorry work in the Unheld Stacks on the Ninefold marginalia. Sorry behaves strangely, and the script responds to it. **Midpoint revelation:** Sorry, frightened by a fire in the Stacks, takes a shape that is not animal at all. It speaks one word, in the movement-script, written in the air with dust: *"mine"* (meaning *myself*). Sorry is a juvenile Vey, separated from its people as a hatchling (or whatever they are) and sold at the Ossel Fair. Ilse's horror: she has been *describing a person* for two years. The ethical crisis of the series. She stops describing Sorry entirely, and Sorry, undescribed, is terrified. They have to learn a new way to be together: co-description, each letting the other help define them, with consent.
3. **The Tenth Figure (Reaping).** In the chapel of the Meridian, a carving of the Ninefold shows ten figures, one chiselled away. Damask's palimpsest techniques recover the faint outline: the tenth was a Vey envoy. The Ninefold made their Atlas *with* someone.
4. **The Moot of the Assembly (Gleaning–Frost).** The Bill goes to a Concordance trial before the whole Assembly: a public map duel between the Court's champion and the College's. The College's champion is Ilse. The Court's champion is Lisbet Arnaud, Kasimir's cousin, now a Court surveyor. The object duelled over is the Margin village of Corrow itself. Lady Reyne's surveyors will chart it fixed, which would make it Court land. Ilse's range-charter keeps it Tenting.
5. **The Lease (Deepwinter–Candlemonth).** After the Moot, with Sorry's help, the full marginal note is decoded. The First Atlas was a **lease** between the Ninefold and the Vey: 1,200 years of held land, after which the land would be "returned unwritten." The term expired in 1200 H. The failing Zero Sheets are the contract running out.

## Central conflict
Ilse against the Court, inside the Assembly. The private conflict is Ilse against her own history of describing and fixing others.

## Character arcs
- **Ilse:** from rogue to advocate. She learns to argue within the law as well as around it. The Sorry revelation forces her to confront her controlling nature.
- **Kasimir:** **breaks with his mother publicly**, testifying for the College. He is disinherited.
- **Cass:** fights for Corrow and becomes its voice. She discovers that the Tenting road-songs contain fragments of the same script.
- **Teo:** gives expert testimony on Soundings that the Court can't refute.
- **Brask:** an elder statesman, at last.
- **Sorry:** from pet to person.

## Climax
The Assembly Moot. Ilse wins Corrow, the Bill fails by four votes, and collegiate privilege is preserved. But in the same hour, the decoded lease is read aloud to the College's Board by Damask. Its last line: *"And when the term is spent, the country shall be given back, unwritten, to those who lent it."*

## Resolution
The College now holds the most dangerous secret in the Reach: the world is borrowed, and the debt is due. Provost Steyne wants it buried. Ilse wants it told. A compromise: they will seek the *original* lease. A lease is a contract, and contracts have terms that can be renegotiated. The original lies in Ordinal, the Ninefold's first city, deep in the Drift.

## Series consequences
The Wending expedition (Book Four). Lady Reyne begins to reach toward the Stillists. Sorry and Ilse form the first co-descriptive bond.
