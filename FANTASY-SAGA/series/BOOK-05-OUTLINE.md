# BOOK FIVE — *THE STILL CITY*
*The Long Survey, Book Five. Spoilers for Books One–Four.*

## Premise
The truth is out in pieces. Provost Steyne and Lady Imogen Reyne unite behind the **Stillist** answer. They argue that if the lease is expiring and the founders cheated, the only safe course is to *finish the work*: to chart everything, fix the Margin and the near Drift permanently, and starve the Vey completely before they can reclaim the land. It means *Total Charting*, which needs massive burden, borne "for hire" by the poor. Tessary becomes the Still City. Ilse's **Errant School**, the students who follow range-charting, is expelled from the Meridian.

## Themes
Certainty as tyranny. The reasonable face of terrible decisions. Hallam's logic in the mouths of a government.

## Major acts
1. **The Schism (Thaw–Blossom 1207).** The Board votes for the Stillist program. Ilse and about forty students and three Masters (Damask, Halloway and Dr. Orle) walk out through the Lantern Gate. They set up an exiled school at Corrow, among the Tenting.
2. **The Great Re-Survey (Greening–Haysel).** Stillist survey columns march into the Margin, charting it fixed. Margin camps are pinned in place and their wagons stilled. Bearing for hire spreads: thousands of the poor go grey to hold the cities. A dark middle. Brine End is fixed so hard that nothing grows.
3. **The Still City (Reaping–Gleaning).** Ilse, Kasimir and Teo infiltrate Tessary. The city is magnificent and dead: frozen fountains, citizens moving slowly through grey streets, and the Zero Stone humming. Lady Reyne herself is half-grey and certain. Kasimir confronts her and almost reaches her.
4. **The Ward of Open Charters (Fogmonth).** The Errants' great experiment: they range-chart an entire Tessary district, the Copper Ward, as an open city. Its streets sway gently; homes are held by the residents walking their own bounds. It works. The Ward becomes a refuge. The Stillists declare it an abomination.
5. **The Zero (Frost–Deepwinter).** The Stillists move to re-attest the Zero Stone with a mass ghosted attestation of ten thousand bearers, Hallam's Equinox plan on a city's scale. The Errants attack the ceremony.

## Central conflict
The Errants against the Stillists: open against fixed, consent against coercion.

## Character arcs
- **Ilse:** becomes a leader. She has to ask others to risk themselves, which is the thing she hates.
- **Kasimir:** faces his deepest fear, stilling, by bearing burden for a dying Ward. He chooses fixity for others' sake.
- **Teo:** **loses his left leg below the knee** holding a collapsing bridge in the Copper Ward. He keeps teaching on a sounder-ivory prosthetic.
- **Lady Reyne:** her certainty cracks when she sees her son go grey to save strangers.
- **Steyne:** the mask of moderation slips into fanaticism.
- **Cass:** a war-leader of the Tenting. She marries Orla in a road-wedding.

## Climax
At the Zero Stone, Ilse confronts Steyne. The Errants pull the ten thousand bearers' ghost-lines one at a time, and Kasimir and Lady Reyne together hold the Copper Ward while the Zero falters. The attestation fails. The Zero Stone, for the first time in 1,207 years, **moves**, by one hair's breadth. Everyone in the Reach feels it.

## Resolution
The Stillists are broken but not defeated. Steyne flees west into the Margin to make one last stand. The Copper Ward stands open. But the Zero's shift is a signal: across the Drift, the Vey are rising to reclaim the Borrowed Country.

## Series consequences
The reckoning (Book Six). Lady Reyne turns.
