- Every scene must change something: knowledge, relationship, stakes or danger.
- Wonder through precision. Humour from character.
- Teach magic through use and argument, never through lore paragraphs.
- Other characters must have their own agendas visible at the edges.
- Avoid every banned phrase in the style guide. Respect the tic limits.
- No narrator flash-forwards. Never leave Hester's POV.
- Vary chapter endings. At least one of your chapters should end quietly.
