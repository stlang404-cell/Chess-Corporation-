# THE HALCYON CYCLE — Seven-Book Architecture

**Series title:** *The Halcyon Cycle*
**Saga question:** What is the Hollow Sea, and what do we owe what we have kept?
**Saga truth (fixed):** The sea was *kept*, with a people inside it, by the Halcyon's founders, to win a war. All grain-work spends that people's held moment. The ending is restitution: the slow return of the sea, the end of salt magic as known, the reunion of two peoples, and Hester's return of her brother.
**Thematic spine:** Keeping versus loving. Preservation versus restitution. What we owe the past.
**Conflict progression:** each book changes the kind of conflict.

## BOOK 1 — WHAT THE SALT KEEPS
- **Theme:** Holding on is not the same as loving.
- **Central conflict:** The charity sleepers' casings are weeping, and the Reader who noticed is "dead." Hester must find out why before her brother's casing fails.
- **Setting developments:** The Halcyon introduced; Ambry; the Cistern; the Stays; the Hush; the first open water on the White in 900 years.
- **Arcs:**
  - Hester: control → stewardship.
  - Teo: flight → listening.
  - Saskia: self → loyalty.
  - Lucan: son → self.
  - Sorle: bitterness → purpose.
  - Orsolya: protector → criminal → redeemed in a single act.
- **Discoveries:** Still runs to quick (applied); venting; quickened grain is living sea.
- **Political:** the Ferrers–Warden contract scandal; Orsolya arrested; Exchequer embarrassed; the Seal shaken.
- **Mystery:** the draw; Aske's "death."
- **Revelations:**
  - The stays are fed by the sleepers.
  - Orsolya.
  - Aske is alive.
  - A living gull.
  - A voice in the salt.
- **Climax:** cutting the draw and venting the Hush quickening up the Founders' Shaft.
- **Resolution:** The sleepers are safe. Aske is returned. Hester is sponsored by Sorle and has passed her Assay. She tells Tib the truth.
- **Saga consequences:** A salt lake on the White ("the Tear"). A living gull. A word: *Aelith.*

## BOOK 2 — THE LANTERN TITHE
- **Premise:** The new water on the White is a sensation. The Exchequer moves to tax and mine it; the Tiders proclaim the Return; Quell sends envoys. The Halcyon convenes a **Board of Inquiry** into the "Shelf Event," with Hester (newly second-year) as its Lowside appointee. The Conclave also proposes a **Lantern Tithe**, a tax on kept light to fund new Stays, which falls hardest on Quell glaziers and Lowside lantern-carriers.
- **Themes:** Who owns the truth? The cost of light.
- **Acts:**
  1. Inquiry politics. Aske recovering. Dunmore's flight traced.
  2. Saskia drawn into Quell–Lumen intrigue (kept light hoarded by Vey's agents to manipulate the Tithe vote). Lantern sabotage.
  3. The Founders' Gallery: Hester, Callow and Aske find the Seal's ledger fragment.
  4. Midsummer Lantern Harvest: the public exposure of the Seal's existence.
- **Central conflict:** Hester and the reformers against Vey and the Seal over whether the inquiry may examine the founding.
- **Arcs:**
  - Hester learns power is not truth.
  - Saskia chooses between the Lodge's money and the Halcyon.
  - Lucan works inside his father's house.
  - Aske relearns his hands.
- **Discoveries:** the Seal; Ysolde's partial journal ("the Keeping was the only mercy left us"). The Held Moment proven by Sorle and Hester's measurement of the Tear's salt crust re-forming *from the past* (the new crust reads as 0 Y.E.).
- **Mystery:** who is buying kept light? (Vey, to swing the Tithe and fund a secret Hush stay project.)
- **Climax:** Lantern Harvest Night. The sabotaged lantern-presses nearly burn the Rookery; Saskia's annealing saves the presses; Callow's testimony is entered.
- **Resolution:** Chancellor Holm resigns and confesses that the founders "made the Ebb" (to end a flood, she claims). Sorle is vindicated. Vey becomes Chancellor by vote, a bitter turn. The Tithe fails.
- **Consequences:** The public now knows the Ebb was made. They do not yet know *how*, or that people are in it.

## BOOK 3 — A MAP OF STILL WATER
- **Premise:** To test the Held Moment, a Halcyon–Lodge joint expedition crosses the White to the Deep by landship, led by Aske with Hester as second. The Wend salt-walkers guide them. The goal is to sound the Deep and find "Ilisse" if it exists.
- **Themes:** Maps and territory; belief and evidence.
- **Acts:**
  1. Departure. The landship convoy. Whiteouts.
  2. The Wend and their songs. Teo learns the Wend chant.
  3. Mutiny and an Exchequer spy aboard. The sink-field.
  4. The Deep: clear salt; the bell-tower of Ilisse seen 60 fathoms down.
- **Central conflict:** survival on the White, and the expedition split between Lodge openness and Exchequer secrecy.
- **Arcs:**
  - Hester and Lucan's romance begins.
  - Teo commits to listening.
  - Aske takes command and is wounded.
  - Saskia stays in Ambry and holds the Inquiry together, a parallel plot via letters.
- **Discoveries:**
  - A drowned city under the salt.
  - Bells in the Roar.
  - The Wend's oral tradition of "the people under the white sheet."
- **Mystery:** What is down there? Was Ilisse real? (Yes.)
- **Climax:** a sink-collapse in the Deep. Hester keeps the landship mid-plunge, a monumental hold, and lags for an hour; the crew vents her down. Through the clear salt they see the tower and *a figure at a window.*
- **Resolution:** the expedition returns with readings and a drawn map of the tower.
- **Consequences:** The Rim learns there is a city in the salt. Vey seals the Deep.

## BOOK 4 — THE DROWNED CHOIR
- **Premise:** Teo, reading Deep grain, isolates speech in the Roar: a language. Hester realises that kept minds are not absolutely still at the casing boundary. Ostrin perception crawls: 900 years equals some minutes for them. The Ostrin *are alive and aware, mid-battle*. Every dram of grain ever dissolved has spent a sliver of their moment.
- **Themes:** Complicity; whether you can apologise across time.
- **Acts:**
  1. The Listening. Teo builds a lexicon, aided by the Wend songs.
  2. The ethics explode publicly. The Tiders riot; the Exchequer suppresses.
  3. The "Choir" project: the Stay Choir is repurposed to *sing* into the salt, to communicate.
  4. Contact.
- **Central conflict:** whether to halt deep mining (economic collapse) or continue (consuming a people).
- **Arcs:**
  - Hester's crisis: her whole craft has been spending people.
  - Aldous dies, after visiting Tib at last.
  - Lucan and Hester become lovers.
  - Teo becomes the Listener.
- **Discoveries:** the Ostrin language; the Ostrin invented keeping. The Marrane stole it.
- **Mystery:** what are they saying? "*Aelith?*" means "Is it over?" They believe the battle is still raging.
- **Climax:** Teo and Hester answer through the Choir. A reply comes, a name: *Vaimé* (an Ostrin keeper-priestess).
- **Resolution:** the Conclave votes to halt deep mining. The Exchequer's economy cracks. Riots.
- **Consequences:** the road to war.

## BOOK 5 — THE RED GRAIN
- **Premise:** Aurel Ferrers and Chancellor Vey's Charterists ally with Hask mercenaries to seize the Shelf and resume mining. Sanguine Grain is used as a weapon: keyed keepings of hostages.
- **Themes:** Order versus justice. Who pays.
- **Acts:**
  1. The Exchequer coup.
  2. Lowside rising with the Delvers.
  3. The siege of the Halcyon.
  4. Ysolde's full journal discovered.
- **Central conflict:** civil war on the Rim.
- **Arcs:**
  - Lucan breaks with his father publicly.
  - Orsolya is released into service.
  - Saskia's Pip is taken hostage in a keyed keeping, and freed by Hester using sanguine knowledge, a moral stain.
  - Sorle dies Settled, giving his last lecture to the Choir.
- **Discoveries:** the founders knowingly kept 200,000 people to annihilate the Ilisse fleet. Ysolde's last line: "We will tell them it was the sea's doing. God forgive us, we will believe it ourselves."
- **Climax:** the Battle of the Causeway. Vey falls (arrested). Ferrers flees.
- **Resolution:** the Rim Concord Treaty of Harl establishes a *Commission of Return*. Lowside is half-burned.
- **Consequences:** the decision to return the sea.

## BOOK 6 — THE EBB REVERSED
- **Premise:** Returning a sea instantly would flood the Rim cities built on the old seabed, and its arrears would be catastrophic. The Commission plans the **Great Vent**: a years-long staged return through a ring of shafts, with evacuation of the lower cities. First returns happen at the margins. Returned Ostrin arrive mid-battle, terrified.
- **Themes:** Repair is harder than harm; living with the wronged.
- **Acts:**
  1. Engineering and evacuation.
  2. The first returned: an Ostrin girl, **Thaelin**; violence; fear.
  3. Ostrin keeper-craft. Vaimé returned; an alliance.
  4. Sabotage: a vent gate fails.
- **Central conflict:** sabotage by remnant Charterists, and Rim fear of the Ostrin.
- **Arcs:**
  - Orsolya dies holding the failing gate.
  - Teo is heavily Settled.
  - Hester fails publicly: a vent she designed floods lower Ambry. She recovers.
  - Saskia becomes the Commission's head.
- **Discoveries:** Ostrin medicine. The bloom fever was an Ostrin-known disease, "the sea-cough," and its cure exists in kept Ilisse physic-houses.
- **Climax:** the Gate of Coldbank holds through Orsolya's last hold.
- **Resolution:** Ostrin and Rim workers together stabilise the Vent.
- **Consequences:** the final Return is possible.

## BOOK 7 — WHEN THE SEA REMEMBERS
- **Premise:** The final Return: Ilisse itself, and the heart of the moment. Stillsalt across the Rim weakens as the moment is spent. Every keeping in the world will fail within a year, so every family with a sleeper must choose.
- **Themes:** Letting go; what is kept *can* be returned.
- **Acts:**
  1. The world's keepings weakening; the Brineries' great choice.
  2. Hester's descent to Ilisse's physic-house.
  3. The Return of Ilisse: the sea coming home over a season.
  4. Tib.
- **Central conflict:** the last Charterist (Ferrers) attempts to keep a last vast reserve of grain, a "Second Keeping." Hester must choose to release it all.
- **Arcs:** Hester's completion: she returns Tib with the Ostrin cure, knowing he will wake into loss. Lucan and Hester. Teo speaks the Return.
- **Resolution:**
  - The sea returns to the Hollow over a season.
  - The halcyon birds fly from the returning water.
  - The Halcyon is refounded as a sea-university, its lower Fathoms flooded by consent.
  - Magic dims: stillsalt keeps no longer, and only the memory of the art remains.
  - Final image: Hester and Tib on the deck of a boat on a real sea, Tib whistling.
- **Final outcomes:**
  - Hester: Reader of the new Halcyon; partner to Lucan.
  - Tib: pilot.
  - Teo: Listener and interpreter, living with partial Settle.
  - Saskia: Chancellor.
  - Lucan: Rim Concord delegate.
  - Aske: grower of oysters, happily.
  - Vaimé: Ostrin Speaker.
