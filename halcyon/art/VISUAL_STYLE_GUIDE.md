# Visual Style Guide — The Halcyon Cycle

## Principles
Wonder through restraint. These are scholarly, atmospheric and symbolic images, not busy "epic fantasy" painting. Every image is original vector work generated in code (`build/make_*.py`). No AI image generation was used, because no such tool was available in this environment.

## Typography
- **EB Garamond** (SIL OFL) for all display and body text.
- Titles are set in letterspaced capitals (tracking ≈ 0.1 em).
- The series line is small caps with wide spacing. Italics are kept for subtitles and captions.

## Recurring motifs
- **The Lantern**: a thin tower with an amber head. It stands for knowledge, the institution and watchfulness.
- **The cross-section**: the world shown cut open, with the surface above and the held depths below. This is the series' signature composition.
- **The crystal cube**: a keeping. Its contents hint at the secret of each book.
- **The halcyon ornament**: a kingfisher-like bird on a single straight line (a wave stilled). It serves as the chapter ornament and series mark.

## Seven-book cover system
All seven covers share one structure:
- the series line at top;
- the title in tracked capitals;
- a dark upper field with a single central motif;
- a pale "salt" lower field;
- the author line at the foot.

Each book has its own hue and motif:

| Book | Hue | Motif | Spoiler-safe? |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 What the Salt Keeps | indigo/ice | Lantern, Fathoms, kept gull (in the cube) | The gull is a symbol to a new reader |
| 2 The Lantern Tithe | amber/umber | floating kept-light lanterns | yes |
| 3 A Map of Still Water | deep blue | a landship sail on the White | yes (no city shown) |
| 4 The Drowned Choir | violet | concentric sound rings | yes (abstract) |
| 5 The Red Grain | crimson | red crystals | yes |
| 6 The Ebb Reversed | teal | rising water lines | title-level only |
| 7 When the Sea Remembers | dusk blue/sand | birds over open water | title-level only |

The covers for Books 2–7 are **provisional**. They are labelled so on the art and are not final commercial art.
