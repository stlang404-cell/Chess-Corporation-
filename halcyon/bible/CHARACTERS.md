# CHARACTERS — Biographies, Voices, Seven-Book Arcs

> [SPOILER] marks objective truths and future-book outcomes.

---

## HESTER VAIL — protagonist
- **Age / origin:** 33 (b. 870 Y.E.). Lowside, Ambry. Daughter of Aldous Vail, a brine-boiler, and **Nan Vail**, a laundress who died of bloom fever in 880, when Hester was 10 and Tobias 2.
- **Occupation at start:** Senior Assayer (uncertified) at the **Ambry Customs House, Dry Harbour**. She started there at 12 as a sweeper and learned the craft by watching. She is paid 14 sols/month, 6 of which go to the Cold House.
- **Appearance:** tall and rawboned. Dark hair kept cropped for practicality, with early grey at the temples. Grey eyes. Burn scars on her forearms from her father's pans. A slightly crooked front tooth. She dresses in customs blue, darned.
- **Sense:** taste and weight. Exceptionally strong and precise, trained by 21 years of tasting grain.
- **Personality:** exact, dry and blunt. Competent to the point of arrogance about practical things, insecure about theory and manners. Loyal and stubborn. She keeps accounts of everything, debts included. She is funny in a deadpan way and is privately sentimental, which she hides.
- **Aspirations:**
  - Keep Tib safe until a cure.
  - Be acknowledged as expert.
  - (Unadmitted) Be free of the debt.
- **Fears:**
  - Tib's casing failing.
  - Being ordinary.
  - Being found out as the person who kept Tib against his wish.
  - Becoming her father.
- **Skills:** assay without peer; numeracy; reading people's lies about weights; a deep, square, immovable hold.
- **Weaknesses:**
  - Poor at release (the letting go).
  - Contempt for privilege that blinds her (to Lucan's quality).
  - Trusts Lowside solidarity too readily (Orsolya).
  - Acts alone.
  - Withholds information "to protect people."
- **Moral beliefs:** A debt is a debt. You tell the truth about the weight. You don't leave your own.
- **Contradictions:** She hates the institutions that exploit Lowside, and she wants their acknowledgement. She preaches honesty and has lied about Tib for eleven years.
- **Private regret (the wound):** On the night Tib was kept, he said, half-conscious, "Let me sleep, Hes. Just let me sleep." She understood that he meant *let me die*. She signed anyway. She has never told anyone. Aldous wanted to let him go, and she overrode their father (she was 22 and Aldous was drunk; the Brinery took her signature).
- **Flawed assumption:** that keeping is loving, and that holding on is fidelity.
- **Capacity for change:** large. She is honest at root, and her craft is about truth.
- **Voice:** short declaratives; trade metaphors (weights, grades, tare, assay); Lowside turns ("I'll not," "wants doing"); dry understatement.
  - "It's not damp. Damp tastes of nothing. This tastes of something that wants out."
- **Book One arc:** from *keeping as control* to *honest stewardship*.
  - She finds out the truth.
  - She refuses a private bargain for Tib.
  - She cuts the draw.
  - She lets the sea move: a release, which is her weakness.
  - She tells Tib the truth aloud.
  - She defers the final decision honestly.
- **Seven-book arc [SPOILER]:**
  - **B2:** institutional insider; inquiry member; learns of the Seal.
  - **B3:** expedition leader; first sees Ilisse.
  - **B4:** the moral crisis of complicity; her whole career has been spending a people.
  - **B5:** war; loses Sorle; becomes a political figure.
  - **B6:** engineer of the Great Vent; failure and recovery.
  - **B7:** leads the Return. She finds the Ostrin cure for the bloom within the returned moment and returns Tib, cured, at 14, into a world 17 years older. She ends at 39 as a Reader of the refounded Halcyon, sea-side. Relationship with Lucan: partners from Book 4.

## TOBIAS "TIB" VAIL
- 14 at keeping (b. 878). Kept 892 at the Cold House, charity ward, casing no. 211.
- A bright, mischievous Lowside boy who wanted to be a landship pilot. He whistled. He drew landships on everything.
- Kept in grey bloom-sickness pallor, eyes half-closed, one hand curled as if holding a pencil.
- **[SPOILER] B7:** returned and cured. He has to learn that his sister is now nearly forty and his father dead. He becomes a pilot on the new sea.

## ALDOUS VAIL
- 61. Brine-boiler at the Lowside Pans (dead salt from Weeps brine). A widower. Drinks, but is not a sot. A craftsman of terse gentleness.
- He wanted Tib let go, and has not visited the Cold House in nine years. He pays nothing toward the fee, and is angry that she pays.
- He gives Hester the pan-wisdom that seeds venting ("you don't stop a pan spitting with a lid").
- **Voice:** monosyllabic, proverbial, then suddenly eloquent.
- **[SPOILER]:**
  - **B1:** partial reconciliation.
  - **B4:** dies (salt-lung). His last act is to finally visit Tib.

## CORVIN ASKE
- 47. Reader in Assay; Inspector of Trade Grain. Unmarried. Lives for grain. He sponsored Hester with his Reader's Privilege.
- **Ch. 2–5:** quick, warm, myopic, hums, bad at politics.
- He discovered three things:
  1. quickened grain leaking into trade;
  2. Cold House weeping;
  3. the draw through the Cistern to the Stays.
- He confronted Orsolya on 16 Russet in the Fifth Fathom at the Old Tap. He tried to shut the valve. Orsolya, desperate, kept him with Hush-grade brine from the stay-line to stop him. She meant it as a pause, not a killing. Unable to return him safely (Hush arrears), she had him carried back to his Cell and staged a self-keeping accident.
- **Legal status:** "kept beyond return," dead under the Stillborn clause. His casing is moved to the Quiet Rooms.
- **[SPOILER]** He is returned by venting in Ch. 25–26 over six days, alive. He is left with a stiff right hand (partial Settle) and has lost three months. He becomes an ally and dry mentor in Books 2–7.
- **Voice:** delighted, precise, digressive. "Oh, that's *wrong*. That's beautifully wrong."

## ORSOLYA THANE — Book One antagonist
- 58. Warden of the Lower Fathoms. Reader in Fathoming.
- Born in Lowside to a Haskish delver father (crushed in the 856 Coldbank collapse when she was 11) and an Ambry mother.
- She rose through the Stay Choir: 30 years of holding. She is **moderately Settled**: cold hands, a slow heart, white hair at 58.
- Unmarried. The Choir is her family.
- **Personality:** warm, practical and funny. She calls people "chick." Protective, with steel underneath. She has no patience for theory that does not keep people alive.
- **Motive:**
  - Quickenings are tripling.
  - The Stay Choir is being Settled faster than it can be replaced; she has buried eleven choristers in five years.
  - She asked the Chancellor for help. She was told to manage quietly and given nothing, because the Seal wants no scrutiny of the deep.
  - She found the founders' Cistern lines in the Brinestacks plans and reasoned:
    - the charity sleepers "are already dead";
    - their families are bankrupted by fees;
    - their stillness can save 3,000 lives.
  - Her clerk **Dunmore** bribed **Pell**.
  - She disposes of quickened grain removed from stays by having Dunmore sell it through Ferrers & Lisk (contract signed "W.L.F.", for the Warden of the Lower Fathoms' office). The money funds Choir stipends and widows' pensions. *She does not keep a fleck.*
- **Fatal flaw:** she cannot let go. She is Hester's mirror.
- **Her "hand":** a widdershins spiral from her left-wrist counter-turn. **Planted Ch. 15**, observed in Aske's casing **Ch. 6**, connected **Ch. 19**.
- Earlier clues: she is seen on the Causeway at dawn (Ch. 4); she says "the Stays have been kind this season" (Ch. 8); she knows Tib's brinery by street name (Ch. 11).
- She offers Hester a private bargain to route the draw around Tib (Ch. 20), and does so unasked.
- **Climax:** she holds the gallery stays so that Hester can vent, choosing rightly at last. She then gives herself up.
- **[SPOILER]:**
  - **B1:** imprisoned, awaiting Proctory trial.
  - **B5:** released into service during the war.
  - **B6:** dies holding the Great Vent's failing gate. Her final Settle is a chosen, completed hold.
- **Voice:** "Sit down before you fall down, chick." "I've buried eleven of mine. I'll not bury three thousand."

## TEODOR "TEO" ANSELM — best friend
- 26. Born in Harl to a farming family. Former novice of the Order of the Returning Tide (Tidewater Abbey, ages 15–24). He left after a crisis of faith: he could not believe the sea would return, *and he wanted to know.*
- He shares a Rookery cell with Hester (mixed cells are common for adults by choice; theirs is purely friendship).
- **Sense:** sound, voice-like. A superb Reader. A poor hold: he is restless, fidgets, and can't keep still.
- Gentle, funny and food-obsessed. He bakes illicit saltcake in the Rookery. He quotes Tider scripture ironically and sometimes sincerely. He is gay, and has an Ambry baker sweetheart later (**Mattie**, B2+).
- **Role:** Reading the Cistern map; deciphering Aske's shorthand (he learned brine-script copying at the Abbey); hearing words in the Roar (Ch. 9). He identifies the gull from a hymn-book woodcut description. In the climax he and Lucan cut the line.
- **Arc B1:** from a man who fled faith to a man who chooses to listen. He hears the word "Aelith" at the end.
- **[SPOILER] B4:** he becomes the Listener, the first to translate Ostrin. **B6:** heavily Settled holding a stay, survives. **B7:** the voice of the Return; interpreter between peoples.
- **Voice:** discursive, warm, self-mocking. "I left the Order because I had questions. The Order's answer was more vespers."

## SASKIA ORME
- 29. From Quell; a glazier's daughter and a glazier. A widow: her husband **Jory**, a landship pilot, died in a sink on the eastern White two years ago. Her daughter **Pip** (Philippa), 6, lives with Saskia's mother **Hennie Orme** in Ambry's Glassmarket.
- Sponsored by Bassano (Lumen). Brilliant and mercenary: she needs money.
- **Sense:** colour fringes. She knows **annealing**, the slow cooling of glass to relieve internal stress. **Key seed for venting**: Ch. 10 (Lantern Night, explaining why the lantern-jars crack), recalled Ch. 21.
- **Subplot:** she has been working paid evening shifts as a courier for the Warden's office (via Dunmore). She carried sealed grain boxes to the Ferrers landship at the dry harbour. She didn't ask questions. She confesses in Ch. 16.
- **Arc B1:** from "every woman for herself" to chosen loyalty. In the climax she anneals the vent: she paces the release.
- **[SPOILER]:** B2 lead subplot (the Lantern Tithe); B5, Pip endangered; B7, Chancellor of the refounded Halcyon.
- **Voice:** clipped, ironic, numerical. "I don't do causes, Vail. I do rent."

## LUCAN FERRERS
- 24. Son of **Aurel Ferrers**, Lord Exchequer, and the late **Lady Celestine**. Raised to rule. Educated by tutors. A superb theorist with a rhythm-sense. He is cold in manner from long practice; he is lonely.
- Bench: Deputy Chancellor Vey's (Brinescript), then Sorle's from Ch. 21.
- **Red herring:** seen arguing with Aske (Ch. 7). Aske had told him his father's firm was buying deep grain illegally; Lucan defended his family, then began to suspect.
- He falls into a sink on the White (Ch. 11), and Hester keeps him. He owes her his life, and he hates owing.
- **Ch. 15:** he finds the W.L.F. contract and confronts his father, who tells him to stay out of it.
- **Arc B1:** from loyal son to his own man. He cuts the draw in Ch. 23, which means defying his father's interests.
- **[SPOILER]:** romance with Hester from B3–B4, a slow, adult, wary love; a B5 break with his father; B7, the father's fall.
- **Voice:** formal, ironic, precise. "I'm told I'm insufferable. I've found it saves time."

## BENEDEK SORLE — mentor
- 64. Reader in Horology. Harlish by birth. Widowed: his wife **Lise**, a Physic, died of the Settle in 880.
- He proposed the Held Moment Hypothesis in 871 and was demolished by Orsolya and censured. He was nicknamed "Sorle's Folly," and has been bitter for 32 years.
- He has two aspirants (both third-years). Acid and Socratic; a secret marshmallow.
- Aske's only friend among the Readers: they shared heterodox evenings. Aske invited him to the 16 Russet meeting, and he was seen near the Third Fathom that night, which makes him a red herring.
- Sponsors Hester in Ch. 14 ("I have nothing left to lose; you have even less").
- He provides the arrears calculus and the vent mathematics.
- **[SPOILER]:** B2, vindicated; B5, dies Settled. His last lecture is held in the Choir.
- **Voice:** long sentences, periodic structure, a sting in the tail. "You have a magnificent tongue, Miss Vail, and you use it for tasting, which is the only use of it I've yet admired."

## SUPPORTING
- **Mother Agathe (Agathe Linden)**, 70. Senior keeper at the Cold House; a lay Tider. She tends the sleepers like a gardener, knows each by name, and talks to them. Ally. She tells Hester early that "the living keep clear, the dead keep grey" (Ch. 1).
- **Jerome Pell**, 49. Director of the Cold House. Underpaid, frightened, bribed by Dunmore at 10 sols/month to "allow Halcyon maintenance on the old cistern taps." Arrested in Ch. 25.
- **Dunmore** (Edwin Dunmore), 40. Orsolya's clerk. Bland and efficient; he handles the contracts and the bribes. He flees in Ch. 23 and is caught in Book 2.
- **Halloran Gage**, 56. Chief Proctor. Ordered by Deputy Chancellor Vey to "lose" the draw-rod and close the Aske file, he complies. He is uneasy. **B2:** he becomes an inside witness.
- **Ilario Vey**, 51. Deputy Chancellor and a member of the Seal. He suspects the deep is unstable and wants no inquiry. The saga's political antagonist (Books 2–6).
- **Perpetua Holm**, 78. Chancellor and head of the Seal. Ch. 25: she sees the gull and is not surprised. **B2:** resigns and dies.
- **Marguerite "Daisy" Fenn**, 41. Hester's customs colleague; comic, warm, gossipy, with five children. She helps with records in Ch. 13–15.
- **Comptroller Mallory**, 55. Customs supervisor; venal; suspends Hester in Ch. 3.
- **Wynn Callow**, 84. Under-Archivist, nearly Settled. He helps find Aske's hidden notebooks in Ch. 19, and confirms that Hush grain still blooms.
- **Hennie Orme and Pip**: Saskia's mother and daughter.
- **Bett Linnow**: a charity sleeper kept 20 years (since age 31). She returns and dies in Ch. 18. Her mother **Ruth Linnow** is 79.
- **Aurel Ferrers**, 57. Lord Exchequer. Appears in Ch. 15 (via Lucan) and Ch. 25.
- **Ama Tessaly**, 66. Choir elder; Settled to death in Ch. 17's crisis.

## Relationship map (Book One)
- **Hester–Teo:** instant friendship to confidant (Ch. 18 confession).
- **Hester–Saskia:** rivalry, then a Lantern Night bond, then betrayal (Ch. 16), then chosen loyalty.
- **Hester–Lucan:** contempt, then the life-debt (Ch. 11), then wary alliance, then respect. A single moment of charged closeness in Ch. 24/26, unresolved.
- **Hester–Sorle:** mutual suspicion, then sponsorship, then real mentorship.
- **Hester–Orsolya:** kinship and mentorship, then horror, then a final shared work. Grief.
- **Hester–Aldous:** estrangement, then a fight, then partial reconciliation.
- **Hester–Tib:** the one-sided relationship with a sleeping boy; the truth spoken in Ch. 26.
- **Hester–Aske:** a 16-day acquaintance turned debt; he wakes at the end.
- **Orsolya–Sorle:** an old enmity. She demolished him and he never forgave her. In the end they are colleagues in the vent.
