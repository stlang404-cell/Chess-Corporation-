# Integration + Line-Edit Brief (Pass 2)

The novel *What the Salt Keeps* (26 chapters, about 127k words) was drafted in parallel by several writers. You are one of six editors, each owning a span of chapters. **Edit only your own chapters.**

## Read first
- `halcyon/STYLE_GUIDE.md`
- `halcyon/editorial/OPENING_TEST.md` (the "Lessons" section)
- `halcyon/architecture/BOOK_ONE_OUTLINE.md` (the whole document; it is the spine)
- `halcyon/bible/MAGIC_SYSTEM.md`
- `halcyon/bible/CHARACTERS.md`
- `halcyon/CANON_LOCK.md` and `halcyon/DECISIONS.md`
- **ALL** files in `halcyon/manuscript/notes/` (the drafters' canon decisions)
- Your chapters in full. Also read the **last ~800 words of the chapter before your span** and the **first ~800 words of the chapter after it**, to check the seams.

## Your three jobs

### 1. Continuity integration
Make your chapters agree with the outline, the canon, the notes files and the neighbouring chapters. Fix:
- facts;
- names;
- dates and bells;
- who knows what, and when;
- duplicated "first" moments (two chapters both introducing the same thing as new);
- re-explanations of something an earlier chapter already showed (cut them, or reduce them to a reference).

Never leave Hester's close-third POV.

Known global fixes to apply where they touch your span:
- **Month names.** The months are Russet, Fallow, Cinder, Rime. "Sere" is only the wind. Seedmonth and Thaw exist as spring months.
- **Endearments.** Only Orsolya says "chick." Agathe says "my dear." Daisy says "duck." Ama says "lamb."
- **Orsolya's father.** He was crushed at Coldbank (856), when she was 11. He was then kept dying at the Cold House for 19 years, and she signed his release at 30. This is canon (Ch. 15). Ch. 20 and other chapters must not contradict it. It explains why she knows the Cold House and Coldwater Street. It does **not** explain how she knows that *Tib* is there; that remains a clue.
- **Ch. 15 and Ch. 17.** In Ch. 15 Hester feels the stay-line "already full" and cold from the east through her hands, but cannot taste through her hands. In Ch. 17 the certainty must come through **taste** (brine on her lips or tongue during the stay). Don't re-stage the Ch. 15 discovery as brand new; make it the confirmation.
- **Ch. 18 to Ch. 19–21.** Aldous threatens to sign Tib's release "tomorrow." Resolve it: in Ch. 19 or Ch. 21 Hester learns from Agathe that Aldous came to the Cold House desk on 19 Cinder, held the pen, and left without signing. Ch. 26 then calls his visit his first time *in the ward* in nine years.
- **Saskia's line** "I don't do causes, I do rent" first appears in Ch. 10. Ch. 16 echoes it; it does not introduce it.
- **The cut** in the Cistern happens at the **twelfth bell** on Assay Night (Ch. 21–24).
- **The shaft plug.** Ch. 23 ends with the plug barely holed (a straw-width hole). Ch. 24 opens with it moving after more work. Make both agree: about an hour of work, loose on one side, a small hole through.
- **Capitalise** "the Founders' Shaft" and "the Well-Cap."
- **Release.** Hester has kept two unreturned beads in R-41 (Ch. 4–5). If a later chapter in your span touches release practice, it may pay this off lightly; don't invent big new business.
- **Aske's Cistern visit.** Aske entered the Cistern on 12 Russet using Agathe's key (Ch. 19 notes). So in Ch. 13 Agathe has seen a Halcyon man use her key before. Make Ch. 13 consistent: she can mention Reader Aske borrowed it.
- **W.L.F.** Do not let Hester consciously decode W.L.F. before Ch. 15. Earlier chapters may show her noticing the letters.

### 2. Line edit and tighten
Bring each chapter to **3,900–4,400 words**. Allowed exceptions:
- Ch. 4, Ch. 19, Ch. 20 and Ch. 21: up to 4,800.
- Ch. 24: keep it about as long as it is.

Cut:
- redundancy and over-explanation;
- repeated beats;
- throat-clearing;
- excess similes;
- characters restating known information;
- travel filler.

Keep:
- the best images;
- dialogue that reveals character;
- every planted clue in the outline's clue map.

Tic limits per chapter:
- "the way": at most 4
- "very": at most 4
- "For a moment": at most 2
- "eyes widened" / "heart hammered": 0

Remove any banned phrase in STYLE_GUIDE.md. Vary chapter endings. Make sure each character's voice matches the voice crib.

### 3. Report
Write `halcyon/editorial/pass2/chNN_synopsis.md` for each of your chapters, in 120–180 words: what happens, which clues are planted or paid off, and what each main character knows by the end.

Also write one `halcyon/editorial/pass2/span_<first>-<last>_log.md` (under 400 words). It should list:
- the continuity fixes you made;
- the word counts before and after;
- any problem you could not fix inside your span, and which chapter it belongs to.

## Rules
- Edit only the chapter files in your span (use the Edit/Write tools), plus your report files.
- Do not change chapter numbers or titles unless two titles clash.
- Keep the header format exactly: `# Chapter N`, blank line, `## Title`, blank line, epigraph blockquote with an attribution line `> — *Source*`, then prose. Scene breaks are `* * *`.
- This is prose for adult readers. Preserve quality. When you cut, rewrite the joins so they read smoothly.
