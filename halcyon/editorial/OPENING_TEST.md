# Opening Chapter Test (Ch. 1–3)

**Method:** Ch. 1–3 were drafted, then critiqued by an independent editor-agent (a reader's-eye judgement with no access to the bible), then assessed by me against the brief's criteria.

| Criterion | Assessment before revision | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | Strong ("fourteen for eleven years"). | Kept. |
| Protagonist appeal | Strong: competent, poor, stubborn, guilty secret. | Kept. Her rationalisation ("keeping is sleep") added in Ch. 3. |
| Voice | Distinct, dry, controlled. | Tic pass: "the way", "very", "For a moment", "went still". |
| Atmosphere / wonder | Strong (dry harbour, tide-marks, Lantern, the cry). | Kept. |
| Originality | Distinctive. | — |
| Exposition | Some front-loaded description (Ch. 2 harbour list; Ch. 3 Pans). | Cut ~40%. |
| Pacing | Ch. 3 stalled: repeated stakes, withheld reveal. | Counting scene compressed to a few lines; Aske gives a real reveal (quickenings 4→14) and a selfish motive (he wants her testimony). |
| Motivation | Clear. | — |
| Curiosity | High (the cry, quick taste, W.L.F.). | Added a concrete detail on the opposing side: a man in Halcyon grey wool oversaw the theft. |
| Dialogue voices | Agathe and Daisy too similar. | Agathe now speaks gently and formally ("my dear"). "Chick" is reserved for Orsolya. |
| Terminology load | Heavy; "Sere" named both a wind and a month. | Month renamed **Fallow**. Proctory and the Line deferred to Ch. 4. |
| Withheld secret | Overplayed by dodging. | The reader now learns "Let me sleep" in Ch. 3, via Aldous. The full confession (she knew it meant *die*) is held for Ch. 18. |

**Verdict:** The opening is strong enough to commit to after these revisions. Word count of Ch. 1–3 after revision: ~14,600.

**Lessons for all subsequent chapters** (added to the drafting brief):
- Use "the way …" similes sparingly: at most 3 per chapter.
- "very": at most 4 per chapter.
- "For a moment": at most 2 per chapter.
- "went still" / "very still" is a magic term here, so use it literally only.
- Flat one-word paragraphs ("Quick." "Warm."): at most 2 per chapter.
- No verbatim repetition of earlier images.
- No narrator flash-forwards ("she would come to think").
- Hold back no more than one secret per scene, and pay something off in every chapter.
