# Ch. 1 — The Cold House (1 Russet)

Hester Vail, 33, walks through Lowside in the Sere to the Ambry Charitable Brinery on Coldwater Street to pay the 6-sol fee for her brother Tib, kept eleven years at fourteen in casing 211. She finds his casing weeping: beads in short lines, all running down and east toward the plinth's eastern foot. She breaks the rule, tastes a bead, and finds it quick. Mother Agathe (who says "my dear") shows her fourteen weeping casings. Pell calls it damp, has recast two with no effect, and wrote to the Halcyon at midsummer. Agathe gives her the rule "the living keep clear and the dead keep grey." Hester leaves Pell a note and steps round the memory of the night she signed after Tib "said something." She sees the *Constant Wren* coming in and the Lantern in the east.

**Clues planted:** directional weeping (east) and the quick taste; clear/grey; the *Wren*; Hester's withheld memory.

**Knowledge at end:** Hester knows something is leaving the casings eastward. Agathe knows the weeping is wrong.
