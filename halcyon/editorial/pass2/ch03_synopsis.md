# Ch. 3 — Spitting Pans (2–4 Russet)

On 2 Russet the bond-shed is empty, the seal cut, both ledger pages razored out. Mallory suspends Hester without pay. Daisy reports a night removal by six men, with a seventh in Halcyon grey wool supervising. She gives Hester the only surviving bill of lading, shipper's mark *W.L.F. per E.D.* in a small, upright, plain hand. At the Lowside Pans, Aldous tells her to let Tib go and reveals he heard Tib's "Let me sleep." Hester answers that keeping is sleep. Pan nineteen spits. Aldous draws the fire instead of lidding it: "You take her off the heat slow and let her talk." Her savings cover two months. On 4 Russet she accepts Aske's offer at Quill Street. He reveals that quickenings have gone from four to fourteen in five years, admits he wants her testimony, and won't guess at W.L.F. aloud. She tells Tib, and Agathe promises to write.

**Clues:** W.L.F. per E.D. (undecoded); the venting seed; Aldous knows "let me sleep."
