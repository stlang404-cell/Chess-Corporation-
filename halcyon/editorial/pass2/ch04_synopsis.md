# Ch. 4 — Below the Line (6 Russet)

At dawn on the Causeway, Hester meets Orsolya Thane walking *from* Ambry with wet sea-wall mud on her boots and bone-cold hands. Orsolya calls her "chick," knows Tib is kept (not where). In the Gatehouse Hester meets Teo, an ex-Tider. She passes the Entry Assay before Crisp and Bassano, keeping a drop crudely and lagging a minute. At the Line she trades barbs with Lucan Ferrers ("Your father's especially"). Saskia Orme calls her "Aske's charity case" and leads the tour down the Stair: chalk names, Callow, 3-17, the Quiet Rooms, the Choir, the Hush door and its hum. From the Lantern a Proctor names the Well-Cap, the top of the Founders' Shaft, never opened. At the Lantern Hour and the First Taste, the Fifth-Fathom grain tastes quick, like Tib's beads. That night in R-41 Teo gives his faith story in brief and Hester names Tib.

**Clues:** Orsolya on the Causeway from Ambry; Fifth grain matches the weeping; the Founders' Shaft.
