# Ch. 8 — The Stay Choir (20–23 Russet)

The sponsor hunt fails. Crisp calls her "unfinished". Bassano is kind but full. Rusk is classist, and Pym and Fane are full; Fane warns that Hester was seen in the Quiet Rooms. Sorle: "I do not take charity cases from the dead."

In her warm office on the Fifth, Orsolya mentions Coldbank and her father, and offers a Choir place at 3 sols a week. A working chorister cannot be sent down. Dunmore's plain upright hand tugs at Hester, and she cannot place it. Orsolya: "The Stays have been kind this season."

On Fifthday night sixty-one choristers breathe and sing. Ama Tessaly ("lamb") names the eastern trunk and the Old Tap. At Stay Eleven Hester holds and hears the sea in the Roar: waves, wind, knocking wood, one faint bell. She weeps salt and lags two or three minutes.

**By the end:** Hester has Tib's fee covered and understands none of the plants.
