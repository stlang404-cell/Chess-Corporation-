# Ch. 9 — The Brinestacks (24–27 Russet)

The Exchequer returns show three Cold House sleepers returned by casing failure and dead in ten months. The century's rate was one every three years. The Cistern plans' card lists Brack (788), "W.L.F. (office)" (9 Fallow 902) and Aske (1 Russet 903). Hester matches W.L.F. to the *Wren* bill, whose hand nags at her. She cannot decode it, and writes to the Registrar.

A Water Board copy shows the Great Cistern under the Cold House and a Main Line running two miles east: "Line enters at 5th Fm." Teo reads a post-fire tablet and hears the falling cry from the *Wren* cargo. A hymnal woodcut names it: "There are no gulls." Leaving, Callow says: "Corvin sat there too… On the first of the month."

**By the end:** Hester and Teo know that a founders' line joins the Cold House to the Halcyon. W.L.F. remains unknown, and Callow's hint hangs.
