# Ch. 11 — The Sink (6 Fallow): synopsis

Orsolya leads twenty-two first-years by the skiff *Gannet* to a bloom-site on the north-east reach. Hester sails for the first time and loves it. At the rail Orsolya talks about her delver father and Coldbank, then asks, "Still at the Cold House?" and names Coldwater Street. Hester told her only that she had a kept brother, and assumes Aske told her the rest (clue planted). At the bloom, the warm crust breathes like the sea she heard in the Choir. A sink opens under Lucan. Hester throws brine over him and keeps him, and the casing fuses to the rim. She cannot release him until Orsolya says, "Open your hand." The arrears snap cracks his ribs. Then she lags for four minutes. She thinks, for the first time, of what keeping is like for Tib. Sorle waits at R-41 and summons her to his study.

By the end, Hester has shown that her release is flawed. Lucan owes her his life, and Orsolya's knowledge of where Tib is kept is on record.
