# Ch. 13 — The Cistern (13–15 Fallow), MIDPOINT: synopsis

Lucan brings a saw and a traced Water Board survey of 398 that shows the cellar grate. Agathe lets the three in while Pell is away. At the re-mortared grating she reveals that Reader Aske borrowed her cellar key on 12 Russet, was below two hours, came up white, and was "dead" five days later. In the Great Cistern a channel of live brine is fed by fourteen new brass rods (Lattice Yards stamp, lot 23) which run from the ward drains to the main line east. Hester tastes it: old keepings, Tib. Teo hears breathing under the Roar, some of it children's. They saw off a foot of rod. Pell attacks, and Lucan's name stops him. He blurts "Halcyon maintenance", a clerk from "the Warden's office" and ten sols a month. At dawn on the sea-wall Hester sees the reversal: the stillness is drawn in, not grain smuggled out.

By the end, all three know the draw runs into the Fifth and that Aske found it first. They do not yet know which Warden.
