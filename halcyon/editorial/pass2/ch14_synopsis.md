# Ch. 14 — What Is Owed (15–20 Fallow): synopsis

Hester brings the sawn draw-rod to Sorle, who warns her not to give it to Gage. She takes it anyway. Gage issues a receipt and warns her off night visits. Two days later the rod has been "mislaid" from a locked store. Gage tells her this is Halcyon infrastructure "above both our heads." Vey, passing, greets her by name. Sorle sponsors her and gives her his worn black cord; she pockets Aske's white one. His arrears lessons plant the third venting seed: "Stays saturate... a stay is a lid." Hester asks, "What if you let it out?" Sorle answers, "Then the moment proceeds," and sets her to work the arrears of a man kept three months in Hush grain (Aske, unspoken). Agathe writes that the weeping stopped for two days after the cut, then resumed, and that Tib's casing foot is thinning. Teo: it can be stopped. Hester suspects the line feeds a quickening but won't conclude it.
