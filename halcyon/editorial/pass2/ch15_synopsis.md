# Ch. 15 — Ferrers (22–28 Fallow): synopsis

Lucan copies a contract from his father's desk. In it the Office of the W.L.F. sells "residual stay grain" to Ferrers & Lisk, signed "E. Dunmore, clerk, per W.L.F." and "A. Ferrers." Aurel repeats his line about laundry and lamps from Ch. 10 and warns his son off. Using the Halcyon Calendar, Hester decodes W.L.F. as the Warden of the Lower Fathoms, Orsolya's office, and at once reasons her way to Dunmore (perhaps working for Vey) instead. Lucan says she is doing what he did with his father. In a private release lesson Orsolya tells the story of her father: crushed at Coldbank in 856, kept 19 years at the Cold House, and released when she was 30 on her signature. She then keeps a candle flame, and the casing shows her widdershins spiral ("My mam said I came out twisting"). Hester doesn't let the connection surface. Orsolya says the residue contracts are Edwin's and Conclave-approved, and Hester is relieved. On the ninth-bell shift Hester feels through her hands that the stay-line is already full, with cold, old stillness coming from the east trunk. She cannot taste it.
