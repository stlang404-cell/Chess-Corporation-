# Ch. 16 — Annealing (2–4 Cinder): synopsis

New Quell boots, late returns with harbour paste on her hem, and a bursary page showing fees paid in full lead Hester to confront Saskia in the Lumen lehrs. Saskia confesses. Since midsummer she has been a courier for "the Warden's clerk," Dunmore, at two sols a run, taking sealed boxes from the Fifth-Fathom lift to a Ferrers freighter. That included the *Constant Wren* grain. The fight is ugly ("you let other people's dead pay your rent"; "Jory would be proud"). Saskia comes to R-41 with Quell clear and they reconcile without apologies. She reports that Dunmore keeps a ring tagged "W.L.F. Duplicates" in his left desk drawer, including the Hush key (black iron, with a bird on a line on the bow). Hester explains away his "The Warden says the Choir eats on this." Saskia rules out O. = Orme and echoes her Ch. 10 line, "I don't do causes, I do rent," before joining "at cost." Lucan arrives and the four form. Hester's ledger now reads only *O.*
