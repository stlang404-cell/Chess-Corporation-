# Ch. 17 — The Silent Supper (9 Cinder): synopsis

The Halcyon re-chalks the Stair names. Orsolya writes the Fathom's eleven Choir dead herself, and her capitals lean left (an echo of her spiral that Hester looks away from). At the Long Table the Minute joins six hundred holds and the candles stand still. Then the alarm rings three and three: a quickening has broken in the Fourth Fathom's east gallery, above the stays, and seawater smelling of living weed runs across the floor. The Choir raises a stay. Mid-hold, stillness floods up the trunk from the east, the same thing Hester felt through her hands in Ch. 15. The stay-line spits brine onto her lips, and the taste confirms it: it is the Cold House line, Tib. The stays are fed by the sleepers. Ama Tessaly holds too long and Settles to death sitting in the line, still singing. Orsolya weeps salt and says, "Twelve," and Hester sees only grief in her. Hester chalks AMA TESSALY, CHOIR, 903 on her own stretch and refuses to think the next thought. She remembers "The Stays have been kind this season."
