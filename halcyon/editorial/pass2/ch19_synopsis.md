# Ch. 19 — The Notebook (19–28 Cinder) — synopsis

Agathe's letter of 19 Cinder reports that Aldous came to the Cold House desk, held the pen and left without signing or entering the ward. On 25 Cinder Hester sits out Callow's silence; he leads her to the Founders' Gallery, where drawer XIV (230 Y.E. fire-salvage) hides Aske's three notebooks and his tasting spoon. Overnight Teo deciphers them: the residue trade through Dunmore and Ferrers; Aske's Cold House visit (3 Russet) and Cistern descent (12 Russet, Agathe's key); the east stays drawing 30 parts in 100 from the Old Tap; and the last entry, which says "She", proposes venting ("a road, not a lid") and ends "tell Hester to taste it." In the Quiet Rooms Hester reads the widdershins spiral in Aske's casing as Orsolya's hand, and the earlier clues fall into place: the salt-mud, "kind this autumn", Orsolya knowing that Tib was at the Cold House, the trolley. On 28 Cinder Callow confirms that Hush blooms within a month.

**By the end:** Hester and Teo know it is Orsolya. Only Hester knows Aske is alive.
