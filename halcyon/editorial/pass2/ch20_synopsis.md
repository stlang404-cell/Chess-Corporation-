# Ch. 20 — The Warden (2–3 Rime) — synopsis

Telling no one, Hester goes alone to the Warden's Gallery at the eleventh bell. Orsolya confesses everything. Quickenings rose from 4 to 14; twelve choristers are dead; in 901 the Chancellor and Vey told her to "manage it quietly". The founders built the Cistern to feed the stays. The sleepers are "already gone." Her strongest point is her own life: her father was a Coldbank man kept nineteen years at the Cold House, and the Guild stopped paying, so she paid from 870. She signed his release at thirty. He came back crushed, called her by her mother's name, and died in minutes. "Did your lad sign his own form, chick?" She kept Aske at the Old Tap to stop the backflow. She knows Tib's plinth, and she offers to route the draw round him. Hester says nothing. On 3 Rime Agathe writes that Tib's casing is dry.

**By the end:** only Hester knows of the confession and the offer. Orsolya knows Hester knows.
