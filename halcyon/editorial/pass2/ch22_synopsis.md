# Ch. 22 — The Midwinter Assay (21 Rime): synopsis

**Events.** The hall is lit with kept spring light. Teo passes with "Assayed"; Lucan and Saskia pass with distinction. Hester names the seventh dish, set by Orsolya: quickened Hush grain from stay nine of the east gallery, "with sea in it." She keeps the moth and then releases it with an open hand. Aldous starts the applause and she passes with distinction. At the Feast, 51 of the Choir's 62 come up and Dunmore's place is empty. Aldous says "Stand to the side." Lucan and Teo leave at the half after the tenth bell, and Sorle and Hester go down at the eleventh.

**Clues.** The release, which she could not do in Ch. 5, now works (payoff). Holm fixes on the seventh dish (plant for Ch. 25). Dunmore is absent (plant for Ch. 23–24).

**Knows.** Orsolya knows Hester has refused her offer and means to act, though not how. Holm, Vey and Crisp know the Hush is quickening. Aldous knows his daughter can let go.
