# Ch. 22 — The Midwinter Assay (21 Rime): synopsis

The Hall of Assay is lit with kept Seedmonth light, and you can hear bees in it. Ambry fills the galleries, and Aldous stands in the top tier in a new coat. The panel is Crisp, Bassano, Vey and Orsolya, with Chancellor Holm sitting apart. Teo listens to his grains and passes with "Assayed." Lucan and Saskia both pass with distinction; Aurel Ferrers claps three times and Pip waves. Hester names the seventh dish as quickened Hush grain from stay nine of the Fifth's east gallery, "with sea in it." Orsolya says that she set it, and that Hester is right. Holm's eyes fix on Hester. Hester keeps the moth, then opens her hand and releases it, the Ch. 21 technique made public. Aldous starts the applause and she passes with distinction. Across the hall Orsolya understands that Hester's answer to her offer is no. At the Feast, 51 of the Choir's 62 come up and Dunmore's place is empty. Aldous says "Stand to the side." Lucan and Teo leave at the half after the tenth bell, and Saskia goes down the Stair. At the eleventh bell Hester goes in with Sorle.
