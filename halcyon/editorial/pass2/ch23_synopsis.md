# Ch. 23 — The Old Tap (21 Rime, night): synopsis

**Events.** Saskia meets Hester and Sorle with the bird-bowed Hush key; Dunmore's coat and chair are gone. At the Old Tap, Hester tastes the Cold House line: forty thornes, and Tib in it. The Hush is a floor of clear salt with a black, ruled shape far below, and Sorle has to be pulled away from it. They work the seam of the Founders' Shaft plug with hot brine, bars and a chisel. At the twelfth bell Hester tastes the draw cut. Jets break from the east wall. After nearly an hour the plug is loose on one side, with a straw-width hole through. The alarm rings and Orsolya crosses the Hush.

**Clues.** Payoffs: the Old Tap (Ch. 8, 19); the founders' feed to the quickening ("a lid"). Plant: the shape under the Hush (saga), and the gull's cry in the wall.

**Knows.** Hester, Saskia and Sorle know the cut has happened and the pocket is surging. Orsolya knows who is in the Hush.
