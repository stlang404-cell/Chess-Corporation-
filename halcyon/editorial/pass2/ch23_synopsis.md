# Ch. 23 — The Old Tap (21 Rime, night): synopsis

Hester and Sorle go down the empty Stair, past AMA TESSALY, CHOIR, 903. Saskia meets them at the box-lift with the bird-bowed Hush key from Dunmore's *W.L.F. Duplicates* ring. Dunmore's desk was easy, and his chair and coat are gone. In the warm Fifth the skeleton watch is singing. At the Old Tap, whose fish-spoked wheel shows recent hands, Hester tastes the Cold House line: forty thornes, and Tib in it. The Hush is a vast floor of clear salt, and far beneath it lies a long black shape ruled with one straight line. Sorle has to be pulled away from it. The east wall is breathing and loud with surf. At its foot is the Founders' Shaft plug, a six-foot lattice disc in a bronze collar. The three of them work its seam with hot brine, bars and Saskia's chisel. At the twelfth bell Hester tastes the stillness drain out of the line: the draw is cut. The quickening surges and jets break from the wall. After nearly an hour the plug is loose on one side, with a straw-width hole through to the White. The alarm rings, three lanterns cross the Hush, and Orsolya calls out to her.
