# Ch. 24 — The Hush (21–22 Rime, night): synopsis

**Events.** Orsolya arrives with Brannoc, Iley and Dunmore. Hester answers her with Aske's "a road, not a lid." The wall opens and black, living seawater pours out. Hester holds by instinct while the pressure climbs. Orsolya sees the shaft taking water and sends her Fathomers to the trunk. Dunmore runs, and Orsolya holds the stays herself: "Open your hand, chick." Hester admits to herself that she kept Tib for her own sake and lets go. Saskia counts, Sorle lengthens the holds, the plug tears free and the sea climbs the shaft. Sorle saves Hester from the rush by lending her stillness, then lags. Orsolya is left Settled but breathing.

**Clues.** Payoffs: the pan's draw and talk (Ch. 3), annealing (Ch. 10, 21), the arrears plan (Ch. 14, 21), "Open your hand" (Ch. 11) and the living sea.

**Knows.** Orsolya knows the vent works and has chosen it. Hester knows the truth about Tib, inwardly.
