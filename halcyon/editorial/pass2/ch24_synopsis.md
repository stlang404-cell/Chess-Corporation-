# Ch. 24 — The Hush (21–22 Rime, night): synopsis

The plug shifts but does not come out. Orsolya arrives with the Fathomers Brannoc and Iley and with Dunmore. She tells Hester she has killed them all. Hester answers that she has given the sea a road, which is Aske's phrase from his notebook. The east wall then opens and black, living seawater pours out. Hester's instinct is to hold, and she starts to close the seam while the pressure climbs. Orsolya sees the shaft taking water. She sends her Fathomers to hold the Fifth's stays from the trunk, and Dunmore runs. Then she holds the stays herself and tells Hester, "Open your hand." Hester admits to herself that she kept Tib for her own sake, not his, and she lets go. Saskia counts the stages, Sorle lengthens the holds, and the plug tears free as the sea goes up the shaft in pulses. The rush nearly stops Hester's heart. Sorle saves her by lending her his stillness through the brine, then lags for three minutes. The quickening is spent and the Hush hums lower. Orsolya is Settled but alive, her hands open in her lap.
