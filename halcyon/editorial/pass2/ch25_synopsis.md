# Ch. 25 — The Gull (22–28 Rime): synopsis

Hester is carried up at dawn. Where the Well-Cap stood there is a steaming black lake, freezing at its rim, with the Well-Cap thrown upside down beside it. In the shallows she finds living green weed, a crab and silver fish, and the ice tastes plainly quick. A gull hauls itself out onto the ice, shakes itself and cries the sound from the Ch. 2 cargo, then flies toward Ambry. The Chancellor watches from a pole-chair, wet-faced and not surprised: "It was always going to come back, Miss Vail. We only hoped to be dead first." The consequences follow. Orsolya wakes, confesses to Gage and is arrested. Pell is arrested, and Dunmore has fled east. Lucan gives the Ferrers contract to the Assize, and Vey calls the night "a natural quickening event." The oldest archive is flooded, Agathe's letter reports the sleepers dry, and Lowside names the lake the Tear. Aske's casing, still clear, is vented over six days. On 28 Rime he wakes mid-sentence ("—you can't, Orsolya, it'll backflow—"), learns he has lost three months, asks whether she passed, and finds his right hand stiff with the Settle.
