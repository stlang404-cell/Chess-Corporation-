# Ch. 25 — The Gull (22–28 Rime): synopsis

**Events.** At dawn a steaming black lake lies where the Well-Cap stood. It holds living weed, a crab and fish. A gull climbs onto the ice, cries the Ch. 2 sound and flies toward Ambry. Holm, wet-faced and not surprised, says: "It was always going to come back, Miss Vail. We only hoped to be dead first." Orsolya confesses and is arrested, and so is Pell. Dunmore has fled. Lucan gives the contract to the Assize, and Vey calls it "a natural quickening event." The old archive is flooded, and the sleepers are dry. Aske is vented over six days and wakes on 28 Rime mid-sentence: "—you can't, Orsolya, it'll backflow—" His right hand is stiff.

**Clues.** Payoffs: the gull (Ch. 2, 9); clear means alive (Ch. 1, 6, 19); Aske's last words. Plant: what Holm and the Seal knew.

**Knows.** The city has seen the lake. Aske knows Orsolya held the Fifth and confessed. Hester knows Holm expected this.
