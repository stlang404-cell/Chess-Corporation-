# Ch. 26 — What the Salt Keeps (29–30 Rime): synopsis

**Events.** In the Proctory cells, Orsolya will not apologise to Hester. "Did it hurt? Letting go?" "Yes." "Good." Aldous last stood at the Cold House desk on 19 Cinder and left without signing. This time he enters the ward for the first time in nine years. With his hand on Tib's casing, Hester tells Tib the whole truth and promises to act for him and to tell him first. Sorle registers her on his Bench. Lucan: "I find I don't mind owing you." "That's a start." At the Ebbtide Vigil the city carries candles to the lake. At the water's edge Teo hears a human voice in the new salt: "*Aelith?*" A sea-wind rises.

**Clues.** Payoffs: Hester's confession (Ch. 1, 3, 18); her two unreturned beads (Ch. 5), now returned; "The living keep clear." Plant: Aelith (saga).

**Knows.** Aldous now knows the truth. Teo and Hester know someone is in the salt, and do not know who.
