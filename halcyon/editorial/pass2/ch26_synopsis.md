# Ch. 26 — What the Salt Keeps (29–30 Rime): synopsis

In the Proctory cells Orsolya refuses to apologise to Hester, and says she would hold the line again. She asks whether letting go hurt. "Yes." "Good. Means you did it right." Aldous leaves his pan mid-boil to go to the Cold House with Hester. He last stood at its desk on 19 Cinder and left without signing. This time he signs the visitors' book, A. VAIL. FATHER, and goes into the ward for the first time in nine years. With his hand on Tib's dry, clear casing, Hester tells Tib the whole truth and promises to act for him, not for herself, and to tell him first. Aldous kisses the salt. Agathe says, "The living keep clear." Sorle enters Hester in his register and tells her she was right. On the Choir Steps, Lucan says he doesn't mind owing her: "That's a start." Before the Vigil Hester returns her two Russet beads with ease. The whole city walks out with candles to the Tear, and Aldous goes a quarter-mile. At the water's edge Teo hears a human voice in the new salt ask "Aelith?" A wet sea-wind blows. Hester keeps her hand open.
