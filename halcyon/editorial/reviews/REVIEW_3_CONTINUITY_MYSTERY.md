# Review 3: Continuity and Mystery Audit, *What the Salt Keeps*

**Scope.** I read Ch. 1–6, 8, 9, 11, 15, 17, 19, 20, 24 and 25 in full, and grep-checked the rest against MAGIC_SYSTEM, the clue map, the timeline, CHARACTERS and DECISIONS D9/D11. I edited no manuscript files.

**Verdict.** The design is fair: every load-bearing clue is on the page before Ch. 19, and the widdershins hand gets three plants (Ch. 6, 15, 17). The faults are in execution:
- the Causeway clue is directionally garbled;
- the "backflow" motive breaks the Fifth Law and clashes with Aske's last words;
- the climax adds magic never seen at small scale;
- the notebook's dates and objects disagree with earlier chapters.

There are 33 errors. Four were flagged by the pass-2 logs and are still unfixed (#5, #8 in part, #21, #24).

## 2. Errors, ranked by severity

### Critical (these break the mystery or the canon magic)

**1. Ch. 4: Causeway geometry.** "It came up behind her, so that her shadow went out ahead of her along the Causeway." Hester walks east toward the Halcyon (Ch. 1: "two miles east"), so the sunrise is ahead of her. The text then has Orsolya "coming the other way, from the city, with the sunrise behind her," yet she "went on toward the Halcyon" and Hester "followed." So the key clue reads as Orsolya coming *from the Halcyon*.
*Fix:* "It came up ahead of her, so that her shadow ran back along the Causeway toward the city." Then: "She did not see her until she was close, because she came up from behind, from the city, walking faster." 

**2. Ch. 20 vs Ch. 25: the backflow.** Ch. 20: "If he'd shut that Tap the line would've backflowed." Ch. 25: "—*you can't*, Orsolya, it'll backflow—".
- Shutting the Tap breaks brine continuity, so by Law 5 nothing flows.
- Stillness cannot run from the quicker stays to the stiller sleepers.
- Aske's words give the warning to the wrong person.

*Fix:* In Ch. 20 Orsolya says: "If he'd shut that Tap, the east stays would have starved inside the hour." In Ch. 25 Aske's line becomes "—*you can't* hold it, Orsolya, it'll only grow—".

**3. Ch. 24: unprecedented magic at the climax.** "It ran out of him into the brine and through the brine into her, because that was the law."
- Lending stillness to a worker in the rush is never shown at small scale (Rule 11.1).
- The water was just called "not stillsalt… nothing still in it anywhere," so it is not live brine.
- Orsolya's hold "across the water" and the Fathomers' "hands on the brass" lack brine contact.
- Hester never lags after her major seam-hold, though Ch. 11 says lag "waited till you'd put the weight down."

*Fix:*
- Ch. 21: Sorle or Rule describes Physics damping a returner's rush with a still hand laid over theirs in brine.
- Ch. 24: add "The sea had eaten the clear floor; it ran thick with dissolved Hush, live brine to the elbow."
- Give the Fathomers "fingers in the brine-slots," and Hester a short lag beat after Sorle wakes.

### High

**4. Ch. 9 / Ch. 1 / Ch. 20: the failures come before the draw.** "*Second quarter, 902: 1.* A man called Abel Corrie." The plans ("9 Fallow 902"), the sleeves ("in Thaw") and the weeping ("Started midsummer") all come later.
*Fix:* Re-date the failures to the first, second and third quarters of 903 ("Three. In six months"). Change Agathe's line to: "Started in the spring, a few. Twelve since midsummer."

**5. Ch. 19: the date of Aske's Cold House visit.** "*3 Russet. Cold House… Told the director I was Halcyon and he nearly wept on me.*" Ch. 5 has "On the fifth… Your Mother Agathe walked me down the rows."
*Fix:* "*5 Russet. Cold House, Coldwater St. Director at his sister's; Mother Agathe walked me down the rows.*"

**6. Ch. 12 / 20 / 6: the trolley timing.**
- Orsolya: "brought him up on a cask-trolley at the half bell."
- Sorle: "At the half after the tenth… The corridor was dark," then "So I went back down to the Second," which is *above* the Third.
- Hester went up before the half and "passed nobody."

*Fix:*
- Ch. 12: "went back up to the Second," and "I came by the Readers' back stair."
- Ch. 20: "We waited on the turn below till Benedek's stick had gone, then brought him up a little after the half."

**7. Ch. 19: the poured-casing clue is restated backwards.** "the casing was densest at his skin and thin at its edges, set from the outside in." In Ch. 6 that is a *self*-keeping.
*Fix:* "smooth as glass at its outer face and frosted against his skin, set from the outside in."

**8. Ch. 21 vs Ch. 26: the beads are returned twice, from the wrong source.** Ch. 21: "one from the Entry Assay and one from the Pans, and opened her hand over each." Crisp kept the Assay bead (Ch. 4); both are Pans beads (Ch. 5); Ch. 26 returns them again.
*Fix:* Ch. 21: "both from the Pans… She left them. Not yet." Keep the Ch. 26 return as the payoff.

**9. Ch. 19: the black notebook goes missing.** "Three notebooks, cheap marbled stationer's notebooks." Aske's working book was "a black leather notebook" (Ch. 5, 6).
*Fix:* "Two marbled stationer's notebooks, and the black leather one, a stub of torn stitching where a leaf was gone."

**10. Ch. 9: Hester sits on knowledge she has.** "Could be anything. Works. Lattice Fund. One of the Wardens." In Ch. 8 she sat with "the Warden of the Lower Fathoms" and saw Dunmore's hand.
*Fix:* Add: "*One of the Wardens.* Something in her went toward the Fifth and stopped, like a hand short of a hot pan."

**11. Ch. 9 / 19: Aske requisitions the plans too early.** "*C. Aske, R. — 1 Russet 903.*" That is before he knows of the Cold House, yet his later draft asks "Cistern??" as if new.
*Fix:* Add a notebook entry: "*1 Russet. Stacks: Founders' Cistern plans, for the east trunk survey. Why from the city?*"

### Medium

**12. Ch. 24: the smell contradicts the living-sea clue.** "something rich and rotten-sweet underneath." Ch. 23 and MAGIC_SYSTEM §10 both say "nothing rotten."
*Fix:* "green and cold and living, nothing rotten in it at all."

**13. Ch. 24: Orsolya's father.** "they brought her father up out of Coldbank on a door, crushed and breathing." Ch. 15 and Ch. 20 have him kept on site, "under the beam."
*Fix:* "the day the Physic kept her father standing under the beam."

**14. Ch. 25: a kept person cannot Settle.** "That will be the Settle. Some of it may come back." The Settle is a worker's cost; returned things resume exactly.
*Fix:* "That will be the arrears. Six days of heat through one hand. Some of it may come back."

**15. Ch. 8: Crisp's age.** "She said it to me, in eight-seventy." Ch. 4 says Crisp is "about fifty," which makes her about 17 in 870.
*Fix:* In Ch. 4, make her "a woman of about sixty."

**16. Ch. 3: Daisy's bells run on a night clock.** "They came at the eighth bell with a dray and six men." By D11 Hester was still at the bench then. The same applies to Marguerite's "third bell… fourth… fifth."
*Fix:* "They came in the small hours, before first bell"; "Marguerite was up half the night."

**17. Ch. 20: the cellar visits.** "I come up through the cellar at the third bell, when there's nobody but Agathe asleep." By D11 that falls in visiting hours.
*Fix:* "in the small hours, before first bell."

**18. Ch. 1–3, 7: the money.** She paid "the six sols and four flecks on the felt," yet Ch. 2 leaves her "the four flecks that were all that was left." Ch. 7's "Seven hundred and seventy-eight… And four flecks" doesn't compute: 24×5 + 108×6 = 768.
*Fix:* Ch. 1: "put the six sols on the felt… 'Six. That's you square.'" Ch. 7: "Seven hundred and sixty-eight. I don't round."

**19. Ch. 11–12: Sorle's build.** "He was older than she had thought… and smaller," with a stick, a limp and fanned hair. Ch. 12 has "small and old." Ch. 5 and 8 say "tall… thin as a heron," hair "tied back with a piece of string."
*Fix:* "older than she had thought him… and thinner," with the string-tied hair and no "small." Introduce the stick in Ch. 8 if it stays.

**20. Ch. 11 vs Ch. 15: the kept flame.** "a falling drop of brine in Crisp's class, a candle-flame, a moth." Ch. 15: "Kept flame… You'll not have seen one."
*Fix:* "a falling drop, a leaf, a moth."

**21. Ch. 16 / 19 / 23: the courier route.**
- Ch. 16: "meet the lift at the tenth bell at the Third Fathom landing… Three nights a week."
- Ch. 19: "after the eleventh bell, two nights a week."
- Ch. 23: the lift "ran from the Fourth to the Fifth and nowhere else."

*Fix:* Standardise on tenth bell, three nights, Fifth to Third, in all three.

### Low

**22. Ch. 19.** "He asked Ennis for the form and she gave it him." Ennis is a young man (Ch. 1). *Fix:* "he gave it him."

**23. Ch. 19.** "Orsolya easy at the tiller." Coyle steered (Ch. 11). *Fix:* "easy at the rail."

**24. Ch. 19.** "*The Stays have been kind this autumn.*" Ch. 8 and 17 say "this season." *Fix:* "this season."

**25. Ch. 19.** "The brine poured over the wick in one smooth movement of the left wrist." In Ch. 15 she flicks wet fingers. *Fix:* "her wet left fingers flicking at the wick, the wrist snapping back."

**26. Ch. 17.** "They carried her up… At the Fifth Fathom landing." That is down from the Fourth. *Fix:* "carried her down."

**27. Ch. 21.** "Saskia showed us, the first day, from the Lantern gallery." In Ch. 4 the Proctor names it. *Fix:* "The Proctor showed us."

**28. Ch. 24 / 26.** "Not Corvin. Not the lads in the Cistern." Orsolya cannot know who cut, and never harmed them. *Fix:* Ch. 24 "whoever you've got at the Cistern"; Ch. 26 "Not the Cold House."

**29. Ch. 6.** "set in a thin skirt on the floor." The casing was made at the Old Tap. *Fix:* "its edge broken short, not quite meeting the floor," which is also a free clue.

**30. Ch. 9.** "The card was written in round black copybook." Whose hand is it? *Fix:* "big plain capitals, leaning a little to the left," which plants Ch. 17.

**31. Ch. 5 / 11.** "Live brine went dead in a quarter of a bell" against "the time it took to say the Ebb-prayer." Canon says minutes. *Fix:* "a few minutes" in both.

**32. Ch. 1.** "a hundred and thirty-two times before in her hand and four times… in someone else's." That is 136 payments in at most 131 months. *Fix:* "a hundred and twenty-seven."

**33. Ch. 15.** "it had whitened her hair at fifty." Ch. 20 has "a woman of thirty with white coming in her hair." *Fix:* "started whitening her hair at thirty."
