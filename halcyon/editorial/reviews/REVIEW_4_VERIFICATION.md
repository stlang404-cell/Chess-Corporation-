**Agency verdict.** It holds. Hester names the gap, bets, calls the count ("Saskia. Count.") and opens first. The release comes from her own memories, so the prompts from Saskia and Sorle read as pressure. One weak point: in Ch. 23:233 the Choir is already coming down, which half-fills the hole. Ch. 24:69 should say the returned Choir is failing.

**Climax magic.** All of it is shown earlier at small scale (involuntary hold, release, rush and damping, tasting the line, lag, heat-jars) except the trunk's **brine-slots** (#15).

## Remaining and new errors (by severity)

1. **Ch. 25:190. Heart warning broken at once.** "Hester went into the release with her hands on the casing under the water, and opened them". This follows Rule's "another rush like that will stop it". Fix: after "Ta," she said. (line 176), add: *"Rule had said small things, slowly, with someone counting. Sorle made it small: a sixth of the arrears a day, a finger's width of salt, his count, and Rule's hand on her pulse throughout."*

2. **Ch. 20:159 / Ch. 19:237 vs Ch. 6:37–45. Trolley timing.** Ch. 20 has "We were on the turn below his Cell when you came to his door". In Ch. 6 she hears them hauling only "a little before the half". Fix Ch. 20 to: *"We were coming up the turn below his Cell when you looked over the rail. I saw your face. We stopped, and turned the lantern down, and stood there with him under the canvas till you'd gone."* Fix Ch. 19:237: change "the tenth bell" to "a little before the half", and "standing still. Waiting, she saw now." to "hauling, and then, when she turned back to the door, stopping. Waiting, she saw now."

3. **Ch. 22:77. Contradicts Ch. 21.** "on the Pans every night while the drops failed to leave her palm." Fix: "on the Pans every night, failing and then, slowly, not."

4. **Ch. 25:130 / Ch. 26:29. Jem Corrie is missing.** The confession has "Bett Linnow, and the three others, by name and plinth" and the apology has "to the families of the other three". Fix Ch. 25: "Bett Linnow, Jem Corrie on the nineteenth, and the three others, by name and plinth." Fix Ch. 26: "to Ruth Linnow… and to Nell Corrie, and to the families of the other four".

5. **Ch. 23:19 vs Ch. 16:77. Box-lift stops.** Ch. 23 has "It ran from the Fifth to the Third, with a gate here at the Fourth," but Ch. 16 has "runs from the Fifth to the Third and nowhere else." Fix Ch. 16: "and stops nowhere between but a locked service gate at the Fourth". Ch. 23:17: "at the foot of the box-lift" becomes "at the box-lift's Fourth Fathom gate".

6. **Ch. 21:159 vs Ch. 16:187. Saskia never went down.** "Saskia, who had carried boxes up its lifts all summer". Ch. 16 says "He never let her go down." Fix: "Saskia, who had met its box-lift three nights a week all summer and heard what came up in it".

7. **Ch. 21:261. Wrong duration.** "She says he was dry a fortnight." Tib was dry from 3 to 7 Rime. Fix: "She says he was dry four days."

8. **Ch. 26:41. Anachronism.** "You'd tasted Corvin's casing. I knew you had." The lesson is in Fallow; she first tastes the casing in Cinder. Fix: "You'd stood at Corvin's door the morning we found him. I knew you'd seen the grain."

9. **Ch. 21:173. Location and formatting.** "I'll be at the Old Tap. I'll taste it go." "And from then…" In Ch. 23 she tastes it in the Hush; the quotation marks are also broken. Fix: "I'll be on the line in the Hush. I'll taste it go. And from then…" as a single quotation.

10. **Ch. 24:175, Ch. 25:184. Saskia's title.** "Give it four, Miss Orme." and "Thank you, Miss Orme." She is a widow, "Mrs. Orme" in Ch. 21:141 and 199. Fix both to "Mrs. Orme".

11. **Ch. 23:45. Wrong stays.** "The skeleton watch, holding the west stays from the Warden's Gallery." Ch. 21:173 has the watch on the east stays. Fix: "holding the east stays from the Warden's Gallery".

14. **Ch. 25:130. Signature.** "the three letters she had signed it with". Ch. 15 has "E. Dunmore, clerk, per W.L.F." Fix: "the three letters Edwin signed it with, on her word".
