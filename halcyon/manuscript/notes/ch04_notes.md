Canon decided:
- The First Fathom hall is lit only by candles until the Lantern Hour bell. The Glaziers pour kept light from the Lantern gallery.
- Orsolya first meets Hester on the Causeway. She says Corvin told her the brother is *kept*; she does NOT mention the Cold House. Keep the Ch. 11 "Coldwater Street" clue clean.
- The Entry Assay panel: Crisp, Bassano, and a sleepy old Reader (unnamed). The kept object is a water glass caught mid-pour. Hester lagged about a minute on her first drop.
- The Hush door: grown salt lattice with a brass halcyon and a keyhole, on a landing below the Fifth Fathom doors. Fifth doors are marked "FATHOMERS ONLY".
- Aske's door, 3-17, has a chalk tongue drawn on it.
- The Well-Cap is seen from the Lantern gallery: chained, inspected yearly by Proctors, and "Lowside children dare each other to knock on it."
- Kept-light posts line the Causeway.
