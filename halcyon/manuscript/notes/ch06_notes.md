# Ch6 notes
New: an unnamed Physic (young woman, red cord); an unnamed porter who found Aske; the Registrar (unnamed, thin, anxious).
Canon decided:
- At the tenth bell on the 16th, 3-17 was locked and dark. At about half past the tenth bell, a trolley with a canvas-covered load and 2–3 people in grey with a blue lantern came *up* the Stair from below the Third Fathom. A porter saw Hester at the gallery's end looking over the rail.
- At first bell on the 17th the door was unlocked. Aske was standing with his right arm reaching and his mouth open mid-word, spectacles on. On the table: an open residue box of Hush-grade grain, 2 oz short. A brine basin was spilt on the floor.
- Clues as outlined:
  - the casing was poured, growing inward (smooth outside, frosted toward the skin);
  - a widdershins spiral grain;
  - the casing is clear, and Gage and the Physic say "Hush keeps clear";
  - Hester tastes the casing's skirt: Hush and quick.
- The notebook and papers are gone. Hester stole one loose leaf from under the blotter: brine-script shorthand, ending in "O."
- Gage is told by Deputy Chancellor Vey (in an undertone), and he names Sorle and Lucan as people he has questioned or will question.
- Orsolya: "You daft man... What did you go and do." She tells Hester to come to the Fifth by the Warden's Gallery: "We look after our own down there."
- The Registrar sets the deadline: 21 Rime.
