# Ch10 notes
New proper nouns: Hennie Orme's shop (green door, two-note bell, top of the Glassmarket); Hallam's works (cheap Quell glass, unannealed jars); Ruth Linnow (introduced here, sitting by Bett's plinth); Ennis absent — an unnamed girl at the desk.
Canon decided: 11,000 lantern-jars this year; each brinery gets one charity jar. Bett Linnow: sixth row, third from end, kept on her side, hands to mouth, haemorrhage; recast Highsummer and 12 Russet; Pell refuses further recasts. Every ward row has a brass channel to a square floor drain by the EASTERN wall. Lucan's evidence: a torn Ferrers & Lisk supplies ledger page: "Cold House. 40 fk. live brine (Deep). Cistern mntce. Acct: Halcyon — per W.L.F." (Highsummer). Lucan's mother died when he was 11; Ferrers House has a roof terrace. Release watched from Lowside's sea-wall top. Saskia pays Hennie with a newly minted sol (courier money plant). Jory: Lodge freighter pilot, died two years ago in Cinder, sink 40 miles out on eastern White, found at masthead set in salt.
Neighbours: Lucan is now a tentative ally; Saskia–Hester bond begun ("I don't do causes, I do rent" said HERE in ch10 — ch16 may echo it rather than introduce it).
